27 nakshatras, ranked by how messy they are

ur rashi comes from a 12-sign zodiac. ur nakshatra comes from a finer 27-part one underneath it, and that is the one vedic astrology actually keeps time with. here is what it is, and how to find urs.

AUGUST 7, 2026 · 9 MIN READ

tell an indian astrologer ur rashi and they almost always ask one more thing: what is ur nakshatra. two tools, two jobs, and most people only ever hear about the first one.

rashi is the 12-sign zodiac everyone knows. each sign is a 30-degree slice of sky.

nakshatra cuts the exact same sky into 27 slices instead, each one 13°20' wide. it is not a shortcut or a spin-off. it is the system vedic astrology really uses to name babies, time events and match couples.

rashi and nakshatra are measuring the same sky, differently

both systems slice the same band of sky, the one the sun, moon and planets travel along. it is a full circle, 360 degrees.

rashi cuts it into 12 pieces of 30 degrees each, named after constellations: Mesha, Vrishabha, Mithuna and so on.

nakshatra cuts that identical circle into 27 pieces of 13°20' each, named after single stars or small clusters: Ashwini, Bharani, Krittika, and 24 more.

so every spot in the sky sits in one rashi and one nakshatra at the same time. think of two rulers with different markings laid along the same edge. they are not arguing with each other.

why 27, specifically

27 was not picked to look neat. it comes straight from the moon.

the moon takes about 27.3 days to go all the way round the sky and back to the same star. cut the sky into 27 pieces and the moon spends almost exactly one day in each one.

that is the whole idea. a nakshatra is the moon's address for the night. if u are tracking the moon night after night with ur eyes, u want one stop per night, and 27 is what that gives u.

this is also why nakshatra is the system dasha timing runs on, not rashi. vimshottari dasha starts from the specific nakshatra your moon occupied at birth, because that's the finer-grained clock the tradition already had.

what a nakshatra actually carries

every nakshatra is owned by a planet. the owners run in a fixed loop: Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, and then round again. nine planets into 27 nakshatras goes exactly three times, so the loop fits perfectly.

that owner is not decoration. it is the same nine-planet list vimshottari dasha runs on, and the owner of ur moon's nakshatra decides which planet's period u are born into.

each nakshatra carries three more labels. a gana, which is its temperament: deva, manushya or rakshasa. a yoni, which is an animal symbol. and a nadi, one of three body types. all three get used when families check whether two people match. we pulled that apart in how ashtakoota actually scores compatibility.

padas: the quarter-nakshatra

each nakshatra splits again, into four quarters called padas, 3°20' each.

this is the level many indian families still name babies from. the pada u were born in points to a syllable, and that syllable is meant to be the first sound of ur name.

27 nakshatras times 4 padas is 108, and each one has its own naming syllable. that is as fine as everyday family astrology usually goes.

why this needs a real birth time

the moon crosses a whole nakshatra in about a day. that sounds forgiving. then u look at padas.

a pada is a quarter of that, so the moon crosses one in about six hours. being two hours out can genuinely put u in the wrong pada. and if u were born near the edge of a nakshatra, it can put u in the wrong nakshatra altogether.

it is the same problem we wrote about for the moon sign, only sharper. rashi forgives a rough time. nakshatra and pada do not.

the receipt

the 27 slices, the 13°20' width and the nine-planet owner loop are the standard classical definition. we have not trimmed it. and the link to the moon's 27.3-day trip is plain astronomy.

one thing worth saying out loud. a few old texts describe a 28th nakshatra, Abhijit, slipped in for certain muhurat work. it is not part of the standard 27 used for dasha and matching, which is what this tool and vimshottari dasha both run on.

faq

what is a nakshatra?

one of 27 equal slices of the zodiac, each 13°20' wide. the number comes from the moon's 27.3-day trip around the sky. it is the finer system sitting underneath the 12 rashis.

why are there 27 nakshatras and not some other number?

because the moon takes about 27.3 days to circle the sky. split it 27 ways and the moon spends roughly one day in each slice, which is exactly what the system was built for.

is my nakshatra the same as my moon sign?

no. ur moon sign, the rashi, is one of 12 wide slices. ur nakshatra is one of 27 narrow ones. ur moon sits in one of each at the same time.

what is a pada?

a quarter of a nakshatra, 3°20' wide. 27 nakshatras times 4 padas makes 108 in all, and each one has a naming syllable attached to it.

do I need my exact birth time to find my nakshatra?

yes, ideally. the moon crosses a nakshatra in about a day and a pada in about six hours, so a rough time can drop u in the wrong one.

twelve signs everyone knows. twenty-seven nakshatras nobody explains. the tradition keeps time on the second set.
