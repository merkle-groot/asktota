somebody at a wedding asks ur date of birth, does some quick maths in their head, and says "ah, a 5. mercury. that explains it."

that is indian numerology, often called vedic numerology. it takes two numbers from ur date of birth, the mulank and the bhagyank, and gives each one to a planet. add a name number and u have the whole system.

it is easy to calculate, so we start there. then where it comes from, and how it differs from jyotish.

the short version

  • mulank = the day u were born, reduced to one digit. it is ur root number.
  • bhagyank = ur whole birth date, reduced to one digit. it is ur destiny number.
  • each digit has a planet: 1 sun, 2 moon, 3 jupiter, 4 rahu, 5 mercury, 6 venus, 7 ketu, 8 saturn, 9 mars.
  • name numbers usually use the chaldean letter values.
  • numerology is not classical jyotish. it borrows the nine grahas and maps them to digits.
  • it uses no birth time, so millions share every number. fun and reflective, not predictive.

how to calculate ur mulank

Your mulank, or root number, comes from the day of the month you were born. If the day has two digits, add them, and keep adding until one digit is left. Someone born on the 28th has a mulank of 1, because 2 plus 8 is 10 and 1 plus 0 is 1.

the mulank is read as ur everyday self: how u come across, what u are drawn to, ur default style. it is the number most people mean when they ask for their "lucky number".

how to calculate ur bhagyank

Your bhagyank, or destiny number, uses every digit of your full date of birth. Add the digits of the day, month and year together, then reduce to one digit. For 14 August 1999: 1+4+0+8+1+9+9+9 equals 41, and 4 plus 1 equals 5.

a second example. 3 march 2002: 0+3+0+3+2+0+0+2 = 10, then 1+0 = 1. so this person's mulank is 3 (jupiter) and their bhagyank is 1 (sun).

where the mulank is ur default style, the bhagyank is read as the longer arc: what life keeps steering u toward. numerologists often say the mulank matters more in youth and the bhagyank grows with age.

the planet behind each number

Indian numerology assigns each digit to one of the nine Vedic grahas: 1 Sun, 2 Moon, 3 Jupiter, 4 Rahu, 5 Mercury, 6 Venus, 7 Ketu, 8 Saturn and 9 Mars. The number's meaning is borrowed from its planet's significations in Vedic astrology.

ur name number

Your name number is the sum of the values of the letters in your name, reduced to one digit. Indian numerologists usually use the Chaldean system, which assigns values from 1 to 8 by sound and does not use 9. The Pythagorean system simply numbers the alphabet from 1 to 9 in order.

numerology against astrology

Vedic astrology calculates where the planets actually were at your birth time and place, giving a chart shared with very few people. Numerology uses only the digits of your birth date and the letters of your name, so millions share each number. Astrology is far more individual; numerology is far quicker.

how to use it sensibly

Use numerology as a light, reflective tool: a conversation starter, a way to think about your strengths, a fun detail in choosing a date. Avoid paying for name changes or big decisions based on numbers alone, since there is no evidence that digits or spellings change outcomes.

  • enjoy ur numbers. they are a quick way into the planetary symbolism.
  • do not outsource decisions. no number should choose ur job, partner or treatment.
  • be wary of paid fixes. spelling changes and "lucky" purchases sold as guarantees are a red flag.
  • want depth? look at ur actual chart. ur moon sign says more about u than any digit sum.

myths, one at a time

Numerology is an ancient part of Vedic astrology.

Classical jyotish texts do not use digit sums. Indian numerology maps the nine grahas onto numbers, a later blend of traditions.

Changing your name spelling changes your destiny.

There is no evidence for this. A spelling you like is fine; a spelling sold as a fix deserves scepticism.

8 is an unlucky number.

It is linked to Saturn's slow, disciplined qualities. In Chinese culture 8 is the luckiest number, which shows the meanings are cultural.

Your lucky number guarantees good outcomes.

Lucky numbers are a traditional association, not a mechanism. Nothing about a digit changes events.

faq

what is mulank?

ur root or psychic number, from the day of the month u were born. add the digits until u get one digit. born on the 28th: 2 plus 8 is 10, 1 plus 0 is 1, so ur mulank is 1.

what is bhagyank?

ur destiny or life path number, from ur full date of birth. add every digit of the day, month and year, then reduce to one digit. 14 august 1999 gives 1+4+0+8+1+9+9+9 = 41, and 4+1 = 5.

which planet rules my number?

in indian numerology: 1 sun, 2 moon, 3 jupiter, 4 rahu, 5 mercury, 6 venus, 7 ketu, 8 saturn, 9 mars.

what is my lucky number?

most numerologists treat ur mulank as ur everyday lucky number, with ur bhagyank as the longer term number. numbers whose planets are friendly to ur mulank's planet are also called favourable.

is numerology part of vedic astrology?

not classically. jyotish texts like brihat parashara hora shastra are about planets, signs and houses, not digit sums. indian numerology borrows jyotish's nine grahas and maps them onto numbers, which is a later blend of traditions.

what is the difference between numerology and astrology?

astrology needs ur birth time and place to calculate where the planets actually were. numerology uses only the digits of ur birth date and the letters of ur name. astrology is far more individual, since millions share each birth date.

what is chaldean numerology?

a system that gives letters values from 1 to 8 based on sound, with 9 left out as sacred. it is the system most indian numerologists use for name numbers. pythagorean numerology simply numbers the alphabet 1 to 9 in order.

how do i calculate my name number?

write ur name, give each letter its chaldean value, add them up, and reduce to one digit. some numerologists keep the two digit compound number too, since they read meanings for numbers 10 to 52.

should i change my name spelling for numerology?

that is a personal choice. there is no evidence a spelling change alters outcomes. if u enjoy the meaning of a spelling, fine. if u are being sold a name change to fix ur life, be careful.

what are master numbers?

11, 22 and 33 in western numerology, which are not reduced further and are read as intensified versions of 2, 4 and 6. indian numerology usually reduces everything to one digit.

does numerology really work?

there is no scientific evidence that numbers derived from birth dates or names predict personality or events. many people use numerology as a fun, reflective tool. treat it like that and it is harmless.

is 8 an unlucky number?

8 is linked to saturn in indian numerology, which gives it a reputation for delay and hard work. it is not inherently unlucky. in chinese culture, 8 is the luckiest number of all, which shows how much these meanings are cultural.
