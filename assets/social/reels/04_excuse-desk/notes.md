# the excuse desk

**not ur fault. allegedly. 9s, loops.**
1080 x 1920, H.264 + AAC.

**Not scheduled yet.** Pick a day and drop it in `schedule.json` as a `reel`.

A fault meter with two ends, `IT WAS U` and `IT WAS THE SKY`. Four confessions
get filed, the needle swings to the sky every time, and a graha gets stamped with
the blame. Then the fifth confession lands, the needle slams the other way in three
frames, and the stamp goes pink: **that was u**.

Beats, at 30fps:

| frames | seconds | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0-6     | 0.00-0.20 | masthead snaps down, 4px settle |
| 8-13    | 0.27-0.43 | the excuse desk bar wipes left to right |
| 14, 22  | 0.47, 0.73 | `not ur fault.` then `allegedly.` on the marigold pill |
| 42-89   | 1.40-2.97 | confession 01, mercury |
| 90-137  | 3.00-4.57 | confession 02, the moon |
| 138-185 | 4.60-6.17 | confession 03, venus |
| 186-227 | 6.20-7.57 | confession 04, mars |
| 228     | 7.60 | the turn. needle slams to `u` in 3 frames, no easing |
| 242     | 8.07 | the pink stamp lands |
| 250     | 8.33 | payoff line and the url |

No voiceover, so a trending audio can go straight over it in the app. The audio
track is SFX only, built from `.blogtools/social/audio/`: a thud on the masthead,
a whoosh on every clipping cut, a stamp on every verdict, and a thud plus a stamp
together on the turn. That mix is quiet enough to sit under a trending track, and
still works if it is posted silent.

Cover frame: **around 0.9s**, on the hook. Not frame 0, the masthead is still moving.

It is a template. The five confessions live in the `BEATS` array in
`.blogtools/social/reel-excuse-desk.html` — swap the lines, keep the frame numbers,
re-render. Nothing else needs to change.
