mars turns up in more readings than almost any other graha, and most people assume that is because mars is dramatic.

it is not. it is because mars aspects three houses instead of one. from wherever it sits, it reaches the 4th, the 7th and the 8th, which means a single placement is touching a third of ur chart.

that structural fact explains most of mangal's reputation, including the dosha half the country has been frightened with.

the short version

  • mars rules mesha and vrishchika. exalted in makara at 28 degrees, debilitated in karka at 28 degrees.
  • it aspects the 4th, 7th and 8th from itself. that reach is why it shows up everywhere.
  • it carries energy, courage, conflict, land, blood, surgery and machinery. force applied on purpose.
  • it is a natural malefic, and also the yogakaraka for karka and simha lagnas. both are true at once.
  • manglik dosha is mars in six of twelve houses. the base rate is enormous and the cancellations are long.
  • dasha of 7 years, about 45 days per sign, strongest in the 10th house by digbala.

the three aspects, and why they matter

Every graha aspects the 7th house from itself. Mars additionally aspects the 4th and the 8th. So one Mars placement is influencing four houses at once, which is a third of the chart from a single position.

only three grahas get special aspects: mars takes the 4th and 8th, jupiter the 5th and 9th, saturn the 3rd and 10th. everything else reaches the 7th and stops.

look at the first row. mars in the 1st house reaches the 4th, the 7th and the 8th, which are home, marriage and upheaval. that is four of the six houses on the manglik list, from one placement. the dosha is not describing something mysterious. it is describing a graha with unusual reach standing in the houses that matter most to a family.

what mangal actually carries

Mars signifies energy, courage, competition and conflict, along with younger siblings, land and property, blood, surgery, machinery and engineering. The through-line is force applied deliberately.

the classical cabinet is a useful frame here. the sun is the king, the moon the queen, mercury the prince, jupiter and venus the advisers, saturn the servant, and mars is the commander. it does not set policy. it executes it, and it is very good at that.

mars is not anger. mars is the willingness to apply force to a problem. some people point that at a gym, some at a deadline, some at their family group chat. same graha, entirely different week.

makara and karka

Mars is exalted in Makara at 28 degrees and debilitated in Karka at 28 degrees. Makara is saturn's sign of structure and patience. Karka is the moon's sign of feeling and protection.

makara gives mars the one thing it does not have: a plan. force with structure behind it is the most productive form of mars there is, which is why the exaltation sits in the sign of the graha that otherwise opposes it most. that is a genuinely elegant piece of design.

karka is the opposite environment. a sign built around comfort, protection and avoiding confrontation is the hardest place for a graha whose whole business is confrontation. the classical reading is not that the person lacks drive, it is that the drive turns inward, which usually reads as frustration rather than force.

note also that both peaks sit at 28 degrees, very near the end of a sign. exaltation degrees are specific, and a mars at 3 degrees of makara is exalted by sign but nowhere near its peak. exalted and debilitated planets has the full table.

the graha that is both malefic and best friend

Mars is a natural malefic, meaning harsh in effect. It is also the yogakaraka, the single most helpful graha, for Karka and Simha lagnas, because for those two ascendants it rules both a kendra and a trikona.

this is the cleanest demonstration of why "is mars good or bad" is an unanswerable question.

so for a sixth of all charts, mars is the most helpful graha there is, and its dasha is often the making of the person. that is the same argument that applies to saturn, and it is why ur lagna has to be settled before anybody says anything about any graha.

the manglik question, honestly

Manglik dosha, or kuja dosha, is Mars sitting in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house. That is six houses out of twelve, so before anyone opens a chart the base rate is already enormous. The classical texts also list a long set of cancellations.

we took the whole thing apart in manglik dosha is a house number, not a sentence, so here is only the part that belongs in a post about mars.

why those six houses? look back at the aspect table. from the 1st, mars reaches the 4th, 7th and 8th. from the 7th it reaches the 10th, 1st and 2nd. the six houses on the manglik list are exactly the ones from which mars either sits in or aspects the houses of home and marriage. the rule is not arbitrary, it is aspect geometry.

and why it is oversold. six of twelve houses is half the chart. depending on which variant of the rule is applied, and whether it is counted from the lagna, the moon or venus, the share of people who qualify runs from very high to almost everybody. a condition that common cannot function as a warning.

the honest reading of a mars in the 7th is a partnership with heat in it: directness, argument that clears the air, and a low tolerance for being managed. that is a real thing to know about someone. it is not a reason to call off a wedding.

mars in each of the twelve houses

Mars in a house applies force to that department of life. Read each line as "this is where u push, and where u are willing to have the argument".

myths, one at a time

Mars means anger.

It means force applied on purpose. Where it goes depends on the house, the sign, and what mars rules for ur lagna.

Manglik dosha will ruin a marriage.

It is mars in six of twelve houses, with a long list of classical cancellations. The base rate alone should end the fear.

Mars is bad for everyone.

It is the yogakaraka, the single best graha, for karka and simha lagnas. That is a sixth of all charts.

A debilitated mars in karka means no drive.

The classical reading is drive turned inward. Frustration rather than absence, and cancellations apply.

Mars in the 6th or 8th is dangerous.

The 6th is one of its best houses, because a malefic in a house of conflict is doing what it is built for. The 8th reads as resilience under upheaval.

Mars aspects are aggressive by nature.

The 4th, 7th and 8th aspects are geometry, not temperament. They are why mars appears in so many readings, and that is a fact about reach.

faq

what does mars represent in vedic astrology?

energy, courage, drive, competition and conflict. mangal also carries younger siblings, land and property, blood, surgery, machinery and anything that involves force being applied deliberately.

which signs does mars rule?

mesha and vrishchika. its mooltrikona is mesha from 0 to 12 degrees. it is exalted in makara, deepest at 28 degrees, and debilitated in karka, deepest at 28 degrees.

why does mars have three aspects?

every graha aspects the 7th from itself. mars additionally aspects the 4th and the 8th. that extra reach is why mars turns up in so many chart readings, and it is a fact about the system rather than about mars being aggressive.

what is manglik or kuja dosha?

mars sitting in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house. six houses out of twelve, so an enormous share of people have it before anyone opens a chart, and the classical cancellations are long.

why is mars exalted in makara?

makara is saturn's sign of structure, patience and long-term building. mars there is force with a plan, which is the most productive version of a graha that otherwise acts first and thinks later.

why is mars debilitated in karka?

karka is the moon's sign of feeling, comfort and protection. a graha built for confrontation is least itself in the sign that most wants to avoid it. the reading is force turned inward rather than force absent.

how long is mars mahadasha?

7 years in vimshottari. it is generally described as a short, high-energy period involving conflict, property, effort and physical push.

which nakshatras does mars rule?

mrigashira, chitra and dhanishta. if ur moon was in one of those at birth, ur vimshottari dasha sequence started with mars.

how fast does mars move?

about 45 days per sign on average, and it retrogrades for roughly two to two and a half months every couple of years. it is the slowest of the personal grahas.

which house is mars strongest in?

the 10th, by directional strength, which it shares with the sun. digbala puts mars at full force in the house of career and visible action.

is mars always a malefic?

it is classed as a natural malefic, meaning harsh in effect. but it is a yogakaraka, the single most helpful graha, for karka and simha lagnas, so what it does for u depends entirely on ur ascendant.

what does mars say about siblings?

mangal is the bhratru karaka, the significator of siblings, and is read alongside the 3rd house. as with every karaka, describe the placement rather than making claims about a real person.
