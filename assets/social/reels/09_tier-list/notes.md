# the tier list

**ranking ur grahas. no one is safe. 9s, loops.**
1080 x 1920, H.264 + AAC.

**Not scheduled yet.** Pick a day and drop it in `schedule.json` as a `reel`.

An S/A/B/C/F tier board. Six chips fly in from the right, one a second, each with a
one-line reason under the board. Saturn and rahu go S, mercury gets demoted to B
(three weeks, three times a year, overrated), venus A, the moon C. The sixth chip
is **ur excuses**, and it goes straight to F in pink.

Beats, at 30fps:

| frames | seconds | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0-6     | 0.00-0.20 | masthead snaps down, 4px settle |
| 8-13    | 0.27-0.43 | the tier list bar wipes left to right |
| 14, 22  | 0.47, 0.73 | `ranking ur grahas.` then `no one is safe.` on the marigold pill |
| 42      | 1.40 | board appears, saturn flies to S |
| 73      | 2.43 | rahu, S |
| 104     | 3.47 | mercury, B |
| 135     | 4.50 | venus, A |
| 166     | 5.53 | the moon, C |
| 210     | 7.00 | the turn. `ur excuses` slams into F, pink chip |
| 240     | 8.00 | payoff line and the url |

Every chip flies three frames in a straight line, then one frame at 1.06 and one at
0.98. No easing. SFX only: a thud on the masthead, a whoosh on every fly, a stamp on
every land, thud plus stamp on the turn. Quiet enough to sit under a trending track.

Cover frame: **around 1.0s**, on the hook.

It is a template. The six chips live in the `BEATS` array in
`.blogtools/social/reel-tier-list.html`. Swap the names, tiers and reasons, re-run
`python3 genreel.py tier-list 09`. Nothing else needs to change. Obvious sequels:
ranking the houses, ranking the doshas, ranking ur exes by their moon sign.
