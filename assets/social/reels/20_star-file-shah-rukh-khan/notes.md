**the first star file outside cricket. solo, four desks.**

## why this one has no news peg

Every star file so far rode something that was spiking. This one does not, and
it is here to test whether the series can run on a name alone.

The hook does the work a news peg usually does: **no film family, no plan b.**
The outsider arc is the most told story in Indian film and almost nobody has
heard it read as a chart, so the frame is new even though the subject is not.

If it holds as well as the pegged ones, the series stops depending on the news
cycle and can be scheduled a month out. If it does not, keep filing off what is
trending and accept the lead time. Either answer is worth having.

It also moves the account off cricket for the first time. Four cricket reels in
a row teaches the feed that this is a cricket account, which is not the audience
being built.

## the downfall desk on someone universally liked

"The charm is the armour. armour gets heavy." That is a reading of a tendency in
a chart and it is not an allegation about anybody's life, which is the line this
series does not cross. On a subject this beloved the desk has to be gentler and
more specific at the same time, or the comments turn on the account rather than
on the verdict.
