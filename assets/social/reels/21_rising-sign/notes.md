## why lagna and not the sun

The comment prompt is the reel. This one asks for the rising sign, which is the
only placement in the set that **cannot be guessed from a birthday**. It needs a
birth time, so answering it is an app open rather than a recall.

That is the opposite trade to `the day of the week u were born`, which was built
for zero friction and gets the highest reply rate in the account. Both belong in
the rotation: the vara reel farms volume, this one farms installs. Do not run
them in the same week.

It also answers a thing people already notice without having the word for it:
that people who just met u describe u completely differently to people who have
known u for years. Those are two different parts of the chart, and the lagna is
the first one.

## the copy rule for this format

Each card is one observed behaviour, not an adjective. "walked in like the
meeting had already started" beats "assertive", because the first one is a scene
somebody can place a person in and the second one is a horoscope column.
