how to read ur kundli, step by step

most people open their chart, stare at twelve boxes of abbreviations and close it again. there is an order that works, it starts at the lagna and moves outward, and it takes about twenty minutes the first time. here is the whole sequence.

SEPTEMBER 8, 2026 · 14 MIN READ

u get ur kundli generated. twelve boxes, a scattering of abbreviations, some numbers nobody labelled, and a second grid underneath that looks like the first one but is not.

most people look at it for thirty seconds and close the tab. not because it is hard, but because nobody gave them an order to read it in.

there is one. it starts at a single point and works outward, and every step depends on the one before it. here is the whole sequence, twelve steps, with the arithmetic shown.

the short version

  • work out which chart style ur looking at first. north indian charts fix the houses, south indian charts fix the signs.
  • find ur lagna. that is the 1st house, and every other house is counted forward from it.
  • find ur lagna lord and which house it sits in. that pair is the spine of the reading.
  • read ur moon, its sign and its nakshatra. vedic astrology reads the chart a second time from there.
  • then, in order: house occupancy, dignity, aspects, house lords, yogas, dasha, transits.
  • finish with synthesis, and use the rule of three: nothing goes in a reading unless three separate parts of the chart agree.

quick facts

What u need
Date, exact time and place of birth. All three.

Whole sign. One sign is one house, no fractions.

Nine: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu.

step 0: check ur birth data

A chart cannot be more accurate than what was typed into it. Confirm the date, the time to the minute if u can, and the exact birth place. Also confirm the ayanamsa is Lahiri, since a different setting shifts every position.

the three ways a chart goes wrong before u even open it:

  • a remembered birth time. ur lagna changes every two hours, so ninety minutes of error can move ur entire house frame. hospital records beat family memory every time.
  • the wrong time zone. especially for births near a border or during a period when the clocks were handled differently.
  • a tropical calculation. if the site is set to the western zodiac, every position will sit about 24 degrees further on, which is usually a whole sign.

how accurate ur birth time needs to be goes through exactly what each level of error costs.

step 1: work out which chart style it is

North Indian charts keep the houses in fixed positions and write the sign number inside each box. South Indian charts keep the signs in fixed positions and mark the lagna. They look completely different and contain identical information.

this is where most beginners get lost, and it takes about a minute to fix.

reading a north indian chart. the top centre diamond is the 1st house, always, in every chart ever drawn. the number written in it tells u which sign is there: 1 is mesha, 2 vrishabha, and on to 12 for meena. from that top diamond, houses run anticlockwise.

reading a south indian chart. the signs sit in fixed cells, running clockwise from meena in the top left corner: meena, mesha, vrishabha, mithuna across the top, then karka, simha, kanya down the right side, then tula, vrishchika, dhanu along the bottom, then kumbha, makara up the left. find the cell marked as the lagna, call it the 1st house, and count clockwise.

there is a third, the east indian or bengali style, which also fixes the signs but arranges them differently again. same information, third layout.

step 2: find the lagna and number the houses

The lagna is the sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at ur birth minute. It is the 1st house. Number the remaining eleven houses forward from it, one sign per house, and write the numbers on the chart before doing anything else.

literally write them on. on paper, on a screenshot, wherever. every single step after this one refers to house numbers, and trying to recount them each time is how people give up.

say ur lagna is mithuna. then mithuna is the 1st, karka the 2nd, simha the 3rd, kanya the 4th, and so on to vrishabha as the 12th. whole sign houses, so there is nothing to divide.

and this is the point where the chart becomes about u rather than about the sky. the same planetary positions with a different lagna describe a different life. ur lagna matters more than ur sun sign covers why in full.

tota says

write the house numbers on the chart. it feels like cheating and it is the single thing that separates people who can read their chart from people who own a screenshot of one.

step 3: find ur lagna lord

The graha ruling ur lagna sign is the lagna lord, and it stands in for u throughout the chart. Find it, note which house it sits in, and note what condition it is in. This is the most information-dense single fact in a kundli.

then read it as a sentence: "my life keeps routing through the affairs of house X." lagna lord in the 10th is a person whose identity keeps getting decided at work. in the 4th, at home. in the 12th, somewhere private or far away.

that one sentence explains more of most people's lives than every sun sign horoscope they will ever read.

step 4: read the moon

Note the moon's sign, its house, and its nakshatra. Vedic astrology reads the whole chart a second time from the moon sign, called chandra lagna, and the moon's exact position in its nakshatra is what starts the dasha clock.

the moon carries the mind: mood, emotional habit, what makes u feel safe. in vedic practice it is weighted at least as heavily as the lagna, which is one of the sharper differences from western astrology.

three things to write down:

  1. the moon's sign. this is ur rashi in the sense most indian families mean it, and it is what kundli matching runs on.
  2. the moon's house. the department of life where ur emotional weather sits.
  3. the moon's nakshatra. one of 27 star segments, and the thing that decides which dasha u were born into.

then do the chandra lagna pass: treat the moon's sign as the 1st house and renumber. anything that looks strong from both the lagna and the moon is a genuinely strong signal. anything that only shows up from one is a weaker one. that is a free second opinion built into the system.

step 5: who is sitting where

List which grahas occupy which houses. Occupancy is the loudest signal in a chart: a graha in a house is directly working on that department of life. Most charts have five to seven empty houses, which is normal.

nine grahas cannot fill twelve houses, so empty houses are the default, not a defect. an empty 7th does not mean no marriage. it means the 7th is run entirely by its lord from wherever that lord is standing, which u will get to in step 8.

while u are here, note anything crowded. three or more grahas in one house is called a stellium in common usage, and it concentrates a lot of a life into one department.

and note anything sitting very close to the lagna degree. a graha within a few degrees of the rising point does the introducing for u before u open ur mouth.

step 6: dignity, or how comfortable each graha is

Dignity is how well a graha functions in the sign it landed in. Exalted is best, own sign is strong, debilitated is worst. Combustion (too close to the sun) and retrograde motion are two more conditions worth noting.

notice the pattern: exaltation and debilitation are always exactly opposite signs. and mercury is the odd one, exalted in a sign it also rules.

two more conditions to mark:

combustion. a graha too close to the sun is described as burnt, its independent voice drowned out. the orbs vary by graha and by text, broadly a few degrees to about seventeen.

retrograde. a graha appearing to move backwards. common, not a defect, and usually read as that graha's business turning inward. rahu and ketu are always retrograde, so it means nothing for them.

and do not over-weight a single debilitation. debilitated grahas are extremely common, and the tradition has a whole cancellation doctrine, neecha bhanga, precisely because of it.

step 7: aspects

Every graha aspects the 7th house from itself. Mars additionally aspects the 4th and 8th, jupiter the 5th and 9th, saturn the 3rd and 10th. Vedic aspects are counted in whole signs, not in degrees, and they only look forward.

the whole sign counting matters. if saturn is anywhere in ur 1st house, it aspects ur entire 10th house, regardless of degrees. western astrology would ask how tight the angle is. vedic astrology, for these purposes, does not.

step 8: the house lords

Every house has a lord, the graha ruling the sign that falls in it. That lord carries the house's business to wherever it sits. Reading house lords is what lets u say something about a house nobody is sitting in.

this is the step that turns a list of placements into a reading, and it is the step beginners skip.

the recipe is one sentence: "the affairs of house A get carried into house B."

so if ur 10th lord sits in ur 7th, career gets routed through partnership: business with a partner, work that is relationship-shaped, or a career shaped by a marriage. if ur 2nd lord sits in ur 9th, money and family come through belief, teaching or a father figure.

go through all twelve. it takes ten minutes and it produces more usable sentences than everything before it combined. the twelve houses covers what each one actually contains.

step 9: yogas

A yoga is a named combination. The main family is raj yoga: a kendra lord (1, 4, 7, 10) connected to a trikona lord (1, 5, 9) by conjunction, mutual aspect or sign exchange. Once u have the house lords listed, yoga hunting is quick.

u already did the hard part in step 8. now just check ur list of lords for connections.

  • any kendra lord sitting with any trikona lord: raj yoga.
  • ur 9th lord and 10th lord connected: dharma karmadhipati yoga, the strongest of the family.
  • jupiter in the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th from ur moon: gajakesari yoga, present in about a third of all charts.
  • mars, mercury, jupiter, venus or saturn in its own or exaltation sign, in a kendra: one of the five panch mahapurusha yogas.

and keep perspective. yogas are common, most charts have several, and none of them does anything until the dasha of the grahas forming it arrives. raj yoga is a handshake between two houses covers the whole family and what actually makes one deliver.

step 10: the dasha ur in

Vimshottari dasha splits 120 years among the nine grahas, starting from ur moon's exact position in its nakshatra. U are always inside a mahadasha and an antardasha at once, and that pair is where timing lives.

everything up to here described capacity. this step describes schedule, and it is the difference between "ur chart says X" and "ur chart says X, and it was loudest between these years."

then do the retrospective check, which is the honest way to test any of this: look at which dashas were running during the parts of ur life u already know about. if the system is telling u anything, it should have something to say about the past.

step 11: what the sky is doing now

Transits, or gochar, are where the grahas are today against ur birth chart. Only the slow ones matter for anything lasting: saturn at about 2.5 years per sign, jupiter at about one year, and the rahu ketu axis at about 18 months.

three checks are enough for a first reading:

  • where is transiting saturn relative to ur moon sign. if it is in the sign before, the same sign, or the sign after, u are in sade sati.
  • where is transiting jupiter, and which house of urs is it currently sitting in.
  • where is the rahu ketu axis now, and which two houses of urs it is running through.

ignore the fast ones for a birth chart reading. the moon changes sign every two and a bit days and mercury moves constantly. those are day-to-day weather, not chapters.

step 12: synthesis, and the rule of three

The last step is deciding what everything adds up to. The working rule that keeps a reading honest: do not claim anything unless at least three independent parts of the chart point the same way.

this is the step that separates a reading from a list of placements, and it is the one nobody can do for u in a table.

an example of the rule working. suppose u want to say "this is a chart about work."

  1. the lagna lord sits in the 10th. that is one.
  2. saturn, the graha of labour, aspects the 10th. that is two.
  3. the 10th lord is strong and sits in the 11th, the house of gains. that is three.
  4. and the current mahadasha belongs to one of those grahas. now it is four, with timing attached.

that is a claim worth making. one placement on its own is a note, not a conclusion, and treating a single placement as a verdict is the most common mistake in every astrology conversation u will ever have.

the same rule works in reverse as a fraud filter. when someone tells u something dramatic about ur chart, ask which three things point there. a real practitioner will list them without hesitating.

faq

how do i start reading my kundli?

find ur lagna first. it is the 1st house, and every other house is counted forward from it. until u know which box is the 1st house, nothing else in the chart means anything specific.

what is the difference between north and south indian charts?

in a north indian chart the houses are fixed in place and the sign numbers move between them. in a south indian chart the signs are fixed in place and the lagna is marked, so the houses move. the underlying chart is identical.

which box is the first house in a north indian chart?

the diamond at the top centre, always. the number written in it is the sign number, 1 for mesha through 12 for meena. houses then run anticlockwise from there.

how are signs arranged in a south indian chart?

clockwise from meena in the top left corner: meena, mesha, vrishabha, mithuna across the top, then karka, simha, kanya down the right, then tula, vrishchika, dhanu along the bottom, and kumbha, makara up the left.

what should i look at after the lagna?

the lagna lord. find the graha that rules ur lagna sign, then find which house it is sitting in. that pair, lagna plus lagna lord, is the spine of the whole reading.

how important is the moon in a kundli?

very. vedic astrology reads the chart a second time from the moon sign, called chandra lagna, and the moon's nakshatra is what starts the vimshottari dasha clock. it carries the mind and the emotional life.

what does exalted or debilitated mean?

exalted means a graha is in the sign where it functions best, debilitated in the sign where it functions worst. they are opposite signs. each of the seven visible grahas has one of each.

how do vedic aspects work?

every graha aspects the 7th house from itself. mars additionally aspects the 4th and 8th, jupiter the 5th and 9th, saturn the 3rd and 10th. vedic aspects are counted by whole signs, not by degrees.

how do i find my current dasha?

it is calculated from ur moon's exact position in its nakshatra at birth. any calculated chart will list ur current mahadasha and antardasha with their dates.

how long does it take to read a kundli properly?

the first pass through this order takes about twenty minutes. it gets faster. what does not get faster is the last step, deciding what everything adds up to, and that is the step that separates a reading from a list.

what is the most common beginner mistake?

reading one placement in isolation and building a conclusion on it. the working rule is that nothing goes in a reading unless at least three separate parts of the chart point the same way.

do i need to learn sanskrit terms to read my chart?

no. about fifteen words cover almost everything: lagna, rashi, bhava, graha, dasha, nakshatra, kendra, trikona, and the names of the nine grahas.

can i read my chart without a birth time?

partly. u lose the lagna and therefore all twelve houses. u keep ur sun, moon, nakshatra and dasha sequence, which is a real chart with the house layer missing.
