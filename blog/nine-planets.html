the nine grahas, and why there is no pluto

seven things u can see with ur own eyes, plus two points where the moon crosses the sun's path. that is the entire cast. here is what each one runs, and what exalted, debilitated and combust actually mean once u stop treating them as school grades.

JULY 28, 2026 · 15 MIN READ

Vedic astrology reads nine grahas. western astrology reads ten planets. the two lists overlap on seven and disagree about the rest, and the disagreement is more interesting than it sounds.

the seven shared ones are exactly what a person can watch move across the sky without a telescope. the two extra Vedic ones are not objects at all. and the three western extras were all discovered after the tradition had already set its structure. this piece goes through all nine, what each one actually does in a chart, and why the words exalted, debilitated and combust do not mean good, bad and worse.

the short version

  • nine grahas: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu.
  • graha does not mean planet. it means roughly the one that grasps, which is why the Sun and Moon count.
  • Rahu and Ketu are not bodies. they are the two points where the moon's orbit crosses the sun's path, which is where eclipses happen.
  • no Uranus, Neptune or Pluto. all three were discovered long after the system was fixed, and all three are invisible to the naked eye.
  • exalted means a planet is working in conditions it likes. debilitated means it is not. neither is a verdict on ur life.
  • combust means a planet is too close to the sun to be seen. it is a visibility fact first and an interpretation second.

why they are called grahas, not planets

Graha comes from a Sanskrit root meaning to grasp or seize. It describes function, not physics, which is why the Sun and Moon count as grahas even though neither is a planet, and why Rahu and Ketu count even though neither is a physical object at all.

calling them planets is a translation of convenience that causes real confusion. it makes people ask why the Sun is listed as a planet, and it makes Rahu and Ketu look like a mistake.

the older logic is simpler. a graha is one of the things in the sky that moves against the fixed stars, and which the tradition treats as taking hold of some part of experience. seven of them are visible objects. two of them are the mathematical points where eclipses become possible, which is a very good reason for an ancient sky-watcher to consider them significant.

the seven visible ones are also why the week has seven days. this is not a coincidence or a borrowing: it is the same observation reached independently in several places. Ravivara is the Sun's day, Somavara the Moon's, Mangalavara Mars', and so on straight through to Shanivara for Saturn. the English names carry the same structure through Norse and Latin.

the seven visible grahas, one at a time

The Sun runs authority and the self. The Moon runs mind and mood. Mars runs drive and conflict. Mercury runs speech and analysis. Jupiter runs belief and expansion. Venus runs comfort and relationship. Saturn runs time, restriction and the delayed reward.

Sun (Surya)

authority, the spine, vitality, the father, and whatever u take seriously enough to organise ur life around. it rules Simha and is exalted in Mesha. it also burns whatever gets too close to it, which is the mechanism behind combustion. a strong Sun tends to read as someone with a clear centre. a strained one reads as someone still negotiating with authority, often their own.

Moon (Chandra)

the mind, mood, memory, the mother, and how u actually process a day. in Indian practice this is the most important placement in the chart, which is why ur rashi is ur moon sign. it rules Karka and is exalted in Vrishabha. it moves faster than anything else, about 13 degrees a day, which is why ur moon sign needs ur birth time to pin down properly.

Mars (Mangal)

nerve, temper, competition, physical energy, property, siblings, and anything sharp. it rules Mesha and Vrishchika and is exalted in Makara. it has a bad reputation it only half earns. Mars is the willingness to start a fight, which is a problem in a marriage and an asset in a surgeon. it is also the planet behind the manglik conversation, which we untangle in the manglik explainer.

Mercury (Budha)

speech, analysis, trade, wit, and the way u argue. it rules Mithuna and Kanya and is exalted in Kanya, the only graha exalted in a sign it also rules. because it orbits closest to the sun it is never more than about 28 degrees from it, which means Mercury is combust more often than any other graha. that is orbital mechanics, not a flaw in ur chart.

Jupiter (Guru or Brihaspati)

belief, teachers, ethics, expansion, wealth in the sense of growth, and the benefit of the doubt. it rules Dhanu and Meena and is exalted in Karka. classically the great benefic. worth noting that expansion is neutral as a force: Jupiter enlarges whatever it touches, which is excellent for opportunity and unhelpful for excess.

Venus (Shukra)

taste, romance, comfort, art, luxury and what u find beautiful. it rules Vrishabha and Tula and is exalted in Meena. Venus is the significator of the spouse in a man's chart in classical practice, with Jupiter playing that role in a woman's, a convention that reflects the period the texts were written in more than anything about the sky.

Saturn (Shani)

time, limits, discipline, delay, labour and the reward that arrives late. it rules Makara and Kumbha and is exalted in Tula. the most feared graha and the most straightforwardly honest one. Saturn does not remove things arbitrarily. it removes what was not built properly, and it gives slowly to whatever was. its 29.5 year orbit is what produces sade sati.

rahu and ketu: real geometry wearing a myth

Rahu and Ketu are the two lunar nodes, the points where the moon's tilted orbit crosses the plane of the earth's orbit. Eclipses can only happen when the sun and moon are near those points. They are always exactly 180 degrees apart and always move backwards through the zodiac.

the moon's orbit is tilted about five degrees relative to the earth's orbital plane. that tilt is the reason there is not an eclipse every single month. the two points where the moon crosses that plane are the only places an eclipse can occur, and those two points are Rahu and Ketu.

the classical story says a demon drank the nectar of immortality, was beheaded, and survived as two pieces: the head, Rahu, which swallows the sun, and the body, Ketu. read as a story, it is mythology. read as a description of where eclipses come from, it is precise. the tradition encoded an astronomical fact in a form people could remember for a thousand years without writing it down.

two consequences that follow directly from the geometry:

  • they are always exactly opposite. if Rahu is at 5 degrees Kumbha, Ketu is at 5 degrees Simha. no exceptions, ever.
  • they always move backwards through the zodiac, at a steady rate, completing a circuit in about 18.6 years. the eclipse seasons that shift about every eighteen months are this cycle, which we covered in what an eclipse does to a birth chart.

interpretively, Rahu is usually read as hunger, obsession, ambition and the thing u overdo. Ketu is read as detachment, inherited skill, and the thing u put down without noticing. they rule nothing and own no signs, which means they take their character from wherever they sit and whatever aspects them. that dependence is why two Rahu placements can read so differently.

why there is no uranus, neptune or pluto

All three are invisible to the naked eye and were discovered in 1781, 1846 and 1930, long after Vedic astrology set its structure. Most Vedic practitioners still exclude them. A minority include them as secondary indicators. There is no central authority to settle the question.

the honest version of this is not that the tradition rejected them on principle. it is that the tradition was built entirely from things a person could see, and by the time anyone found the outer three the framework had been stable for well over a thousand years.

the argument for keeping them out is coherence. every technique in jyotish, the dashas, the aspects, the dignities, the yogas, is calibrated for nine grahas. adding three more does not extend the system so much as break its arithmetic. Vimshottari dasha allocates 120 years across nine planets. there is no room in that structure for a tenth.

the argument for adding them is observational. some modern practitioners find Uranus useful for sudden disruption and Neptune for dissolution. they use them as commentary on top of a nine-graha reading rather than as full participants.

we stay with nine. it is the standard, it keeps the dasha arithmetic intact, and the tradition already has Rahu for disruption and Ketu for dissolution, which is roughly the job people hand to the outer planets anyway.

exalted, debilitated, own sign: what dignity actually means

Dignity describes the working conditions a planet finds itself in, not the quality of ur life. Exalted means the sign amplifies what that graha does best. Debilitated means it does not. A debilitated planet usually reads as effort and late competence rather than failure.

the school-grade reading of dignity is the single most common beginner error in this subject. exalted does not mean good and debilitated does not mean doomed.

Rahu and Ketu have no agreed exaltation, and the sources genuinely disagree, so anyone stating one confidently is picking a side without telling u.

there is also neecha bhanga, cancellation of debilitation, a set of classical conditions under which a debilitated planet recovers and sometimes overperforms. the concept exists precisely because the tradition never intended debilitation as a life sentence.

combust and retrograde, without the drama

Combust means a planet is close enough to the sun to be lost in its glare, which is a visibility fact before it is an interpretation. Retrograde means a planet appears to move backwards from earth, which is a perspective effect caused by relative orbital speeds, not a change in direction.

combustion

when a graha sits within a certain number of degrees of the sun, u cannot see it. that is the whole physical fact. the traditional orbs are Moon 12 degrees, Mars 17, Mercury 14, Jupiter 11, Venus 10, and Saturn 15.

because Mercury never gets more than about 28 degrees from the sun and Venus never more than about 47, both are combust far more often than the outer planets. that is orbital mechanics rather than a statement about ur chart, and any reading that treats a combust Mercury as remarkable has not done the arithmetic on how common it is.

interpretively, a combust planet is usually read as one whose agenda gets absorbed into the Sun's. not destroyed. subordinated.

retrogression

no planet actually reverses. retrogression is what u see when earth overtakes a slower outer planet on the inside of the track, the same way a slower train appears to slide backwards from a faster one. the effect is entirely a matter of viewing angle.

it is also far more common than people assume. across any ten year window, Saturn spends about 37 percent of its time retrograde, Jupiter about 30 percent, Mercury about 19 percent, Mars about 10 percent, and Venus about 7 percent. Rahu and Ketu are retrograde permanently by definition.

Vedic and western practice differ here. western astrology tends to read retrogression as internalised or delayed expression. some classical Vedic sources treat a retrograde planet as unusually strong, on the grounds that it is closest to earth and brightest at that point, which is observably true. we report the fact and flag that the interpretation is contested rather than pretending it is settled.

planetary friendships, briefly

Vedic astrology classifies each graha's relationship to every other as friend, neutral or enemy, and uses that to judge how comfortable a planet is in a sign ruled by another. There is also a temporary friendship layer based on how close together two planets sit in a given chart.

the pattern is not arbitrary once u look at it. the luminaries and Mars cluster together, and Venus and Saturn cluster on the other side. the tradition is grouping by function: the hot, assertive, personal grahas against the cool, structural, impersonal ones.

on top of this permanent scheme sits a temporary one, based on how many signs apart two planets are in the specific chart being read. combining both gives five grades from great friend to great enemy, which is one of the ways practitioners weigh a placement without reaching for a calculator.

what gets repeated, and what is actually true

A debilitated planet ruins that part of my life.

It describes a planet working in conditions that do not suit it, which usually reads as effort and late competence. The tradition also has neecha bhanga, a formal set of conditions that cancel debilitation entirely, which would make no sense if debilitation were fatal.

Saturn is a malefic, so Saturn placements are bad news.

Saturn restricts, delays and demands proper work. That is unpleasant and frequently useful. The classical texts treat it as the great teacher, which is a different claim from the great punisher.

Rahu and Ketu are demons in my chart.

They are the two points where the moon's orbit crosses the sun's path. The demon is a mnemonic for eclipse geometry, and a good one, because it survived several thousand years.

Mercury retrograde will break my plans.

Mercury is retrograde about 19 percent of the time, which is roughly one day in five of ur entire life. Any effect that consistent would be indistinguishable from normal.

Vedic astrology ignores Pluto because it is not a real planet.

It ignores Pluto because Pluto was found in 1930, roughly two thousand years after the system fixed its structure around what the eye can see. The 2006 reclassification had nothing to do with it.

An exalted planet guarantees success in that area.

Exaltation is strongest at one exact degree and fades from there. A planet exalted at the far end of its sign is only nominally exalted, and dignity says nothing at all about which houses that planet rules.

mistakes worth not making

  • reading dignity as a grade. exalted and debilitated describe conditions. they do not describe outcomes and they do not outrank house lordship.
  • panicking about combust Mercury. Mercury never strays more than about 28 degrees from the sun. combustion is its default state, not an event.
  • treating Rahu and Ketu as independent actors. they own no signs and take their entire character from where they sit and what aspects them.
  • importing western planet meanings wholesale. Venus in Vedic practice covers comfort, vehicles and material ease as much as romance, and Saturn covers labour as much as karma.
  • ignoring speed. a moon placement is shared by everyone born within about two days of u. a lagna placement is shared by people born within two hours. weight them accordingly.

tota says

the fastest way to stop being frightened by a chart is to learn what combustion is. Mercury is combust in something like a quarter of all charts because it physically cannot get far from the sun. once u see that one label is mostly orbital mechanics, u start asking the same question of every other scary word on the page.

the receipt

the exaltation degrees, sign rulerships and permanent friendship scheme here follow Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, cross-checked against Phaladeepika. the combustion orbs given are the widely used Vedic set and do vary a little between sources, which is worth knowing before anyone quotes one at u as fixed.

the retrograde percentages quoted are not traditional claims. we computed them by stepping through 3,650 consecutive days from 2020 and measuring apparent daily motion directly from the orbital geometry: Saturn 37.4 percent, Jupiter 29.6 percent, Mercury 19.0 percent, Mars 10.4 percent, Venus 7.3 percent. the maximum elongations quoted for Mercury and Venus, about 28 and 47 degrees, came out of the same calculation and match the published values.

the eclipse geometry behind Rahu and Ketu, a lunar orbital inclination of about 5 degrees and a nodal cycle of about 18.6 years, is standard astronomy.

frequently asked questions

what are the 9 planets in Vedic astrology?

Sun (Surya), Moon (Chandra), Mars (Mangal), Mercury (Budha), Jupiter (Guru), Venus (Shukra), Saturn (Shani), Rahu and Ketu. Together they are called the navagraha.

why are there only 9 planets in Vedic astrology?

because the system was built from what the naked eye can see, plus the two eclipse points. Uranus, Neptune and Pluto were discovered in 1781, 1846 and 1930, long after the structure was fixed.

what are Rahu and Ketu?

the two points where the moon's orbit crosses the plane of the earth's orbit. eclipses can only happen near them. they are not physical objects, they are always exactly opposite each other, and they always move backwards.

which is the strongest planet in my chart?

it depends on ur chart. the usual candidates are ur lagna lord, whichever graha is exalted or in its own sign, and whichever one rules ur current dasha. there is no universal answer.

what does exalted mean?

the planet is in the sign where its qualities express most easily. the effect is strongest at one exact degree and weakens away from it, so a nominally exalted planet at the far end of its sign is much less exalted than the word suggests.

what does debilitated mean?

the planet is in the sign opposite its exaltation, so it is working in conditions that do not suit it. it usually reads as effort and late competence, and the tradition has a formal set of conditions, neecha bhanga, that cancel it.

what does combust mean in astrology?

the planet is close enough to the sun to be lost in its glare and invisible from earth. the orbs vary by planet, from 10 degrees for Venus to 17 for Mars.

is retrograde bad?

no planet actually reverses. retrogression is a perspective effect from earth overtaking a slower planet. Saturn is retrograde about 37 percent of the time, so treating it as an emergency would mean treating a third of ur life as one.

which planet is the most malefic?

Saturn and Mars are the classical natural malefics, with Rahu and Ketu usually added. malefic in this tradition means difficult and demanding, not evil.

which planet is responsible for marriage?

Venus is the general significator of relationships, Jupiter of the husband in classical readings of a woman's chart, and the 7th house and its lord carry more weight than either. Mars is the one behind the manglik conversation.

which planet controls money?

the 2nd and 11th houses and their lords do most of the work. Jupiter is associated with growth and abundance, and Venus with material comfort, but house lordship matters more than any single significator.

why is Mercury combust so often?

because it orbits closer to the sun than earth does and never appears more than about 28 degrees away from it. combustion is close to its normal condition.

do Vedic astrologers use Uranus, Neptune and Pluto?

most do not. a minority use them as secondary commentary. the core techniques, especially Vimshottari dasha with its 120 years split across nine grahas, are calibrated for nine and do not extend cleanly.

what is a natural benefic and malefic?

Jupiter, Venus, a waxing Moon and an unafflicted Mercury are natural benefics. Saturn, Mars, a waning Moon, Rahu and Ketu are natural malefics. house lordship in a specific chart can override both.

what does my strongest planet mean for me?

in practice, whichever graha rules ur current dasha is the one shaping the chapter u are in, regardless of which is technically strongest. that is usually the more useful question to ask.

the five things worth remembering

  1. nine grahas, seven of them visible to the naked eye and two of them eclipse points. that is the whole cast, and the outer planets are absent for historical reasons rather than doctrinal ones.
  2. Rahu and Ketu are always exactly opposite, always retrograde, and own no signs, so they take their character entirely from context.
  3. dignity is working conditions, not a grade. debilitation has a formal cancellation rule precisely because it was never meant as a life sentence.
  4. combustion is a visibility fact. Mercury is combust constantly because it physically cannot get far from the sun.
  5. retrogression is a viewing angle. Saturn spends over a third of its time retrograde, so it cannot mean what the panic industry says it means.
