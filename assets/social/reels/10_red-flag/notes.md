# red flag / green flag

**ur chart. flagged. no appeal. 9s, loops.**
1080 x 1920, H.264 + AAC.

**Not scheduled yet.** Pick a day and drop it in `schedule.json` as a `reel`.

Five placement cards drop in one after another. Each gets a stamp, RED FLAG in pink
or GREEN FLAG in parrot green, then gets shoved into the pile behind, tilted, as the
next one lands. Venus in the 12th, moon in the 4th, mars in the 7th, jupiter in the
2nd. The fifth card is **u, reading this, instead of texting back**, and it gets a
marigold green flag.

Beats, at 30fps:

| frames | seconds | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0-6     | 0.00-0.20 | masthead snaps down, 4px settle |
| 8-13    | 0.27-0.43 | the placements bar wipes left to right |
| 14, 22  | 0.47, 0.73 | `ur chart.` then `flagged.` on a pink pill |
| 42, 60  | 1.40, 2.00 | venus in the 12th lands, red flag |
| 84, 102 | 2.80, 3.40 | moon in the 4th, green flag |
| 126, 144 | 4.20, 4.80 | mars in the 7th, red flag |
| 168, 186 | 5.60, 6.20 | jupiter in the 2nd, green flag |
| 210, 228 | 7.00, 7.60 | the turn. `u, reading this` lands, marigold green flag |
| 246     | 8.20 | payoff line and the url |

Cards drop 60px over four frames, hard. Stamps land one frame at 1.14, one at 0.96,
then hold, six degrees off square. SFX only: thud on the masthead, whoosh on every
card, stamp on every verdict.

Cover frame: **around 2.0s**, the first stamp.

It is a template. The five cards live in the `BEATS` array in
`.blogtools/social/reel-red-flag.html`; `v` is `red`, `green` or `gold`. Swap and
re-run `python3 genreel.py red-flag 10`. Sequels: red flag / green flag for
transits, for nakshatras, for the person they are dating.
