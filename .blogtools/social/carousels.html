cont. p.2 →
the setup

first: which chart is it?

north indian fixes the houses and moves the sign numbers between them. south indian fixes the signs and marks the lagna. same chart, two layouts, and this is where most people get lost.

cont. p.3 →
1step one

find ur lagna

the sign rising at ur exact birth minute. it becomes the 1st house. write all twelve house numbers on the chart before doing anything else. this is the step people skip.

cont. p.4 →
2step two

find ur lagna lord

the graha ruling that sign stands in for u. whichever house it sits in is where ur life keeps happening, whether or not u planned it that way.

cont. p.5 →
3step three

read the moon

sign, house, nakshatra. then read the whole chart again from the moon sign. that is a free second opinion built into the system, and vedic astrology uses it constantly.

cont. p.6 →
4–7the middle

occupancy, dignity, aspects, lords

who sits where. how comfortable each one is. what each aspects. then the big one: place every house lord and write twelve sentences of “house A gets carried into house B”.

cont. p.7 →
8–11the timing

yogas say what. dashas say when.

a yoga is capacity. the dasha is the schedule. nothing in a chart fires outside its own period, which is why most people's “raj yoga” has never done anything for them.

cont. p.8 →
12step twelve

the rule of three

never claim anything unless three separate parts of the chart point the same way. it keeps ur reading honest, and it is the best fraud filter u will ever own.

mon, 21 septthe sky deskvol. u

two of ur nine planets don't exist

no body. no light. no mass. nothing to photograph. and the tradition weights them harder than almost anything.

swipe →
cont. p.2 →
the setup

picture two hula hoops

hold one flat. that is the sun's path. tilt the second one about five degrees and push it through. that is the moon's path. they meet in exactly two places.

cont. p.3 →
what they are

those two places are rahu and ketu

the lunar nodes. and because two circles that pass through the same centre cross on opposite sides, they are always exactly 180 degrees apart. in every chart ever cast.

cont. p.4 →
the eclipse

eclipses only happen near them

that five degree tilt is the entire reason we do not get an eclipse every single month. the nodes are the only two doors an eclipse can come through.

cont. p.5 →
the story

the myth is astronomy in disguise

a severed head and a headless body, permanently opposite, forever chasing the sun and the moon, only able to catch them at two specific points. every detail is a true statement about the nodes.

cont. p.6 →
rahu

appetite with no off switch

a head with no stomach. it consumes and is never full. where rahu sits is where u chase, where u overreach, and where u are most likely to be spectacular and least likely to be sensible.

cont. p.7 →
ketu

the thing u already finished

a body with no head. acts without appetite. where u are unusually good at something and get absolutely nothing out of being good at it.

cont. p.8 →
the axis

they always sit opposite

so u are obsessed with one department of ur life and bored of the exact opposite one. that pairing is the reading. neither placement means much alone.

“we got 28 out of 36”

said constantly. almost nobody saying it can name the eight things being checked. here they are.

swipe →
cont. p.2 →
the setup

eight checks, run on two moons

guna milan compares the two birth nakshatras and the two moon signs. that is all it looks at. no lagna, no houses, no dasha. eight kootas, scored 1 through 8, adding to 36.

cont. p.3 →
1 · 2 · 3 points

varna, vashya, tara

temperament grouping, mutual influence, and birth-star compatibility counted between the two nakshatras. six points between them, and the three least quoted items on the list.

cont. p.4 →
4 points

yoni

physical compatibility, scored through the animal symbol attached to each nakshatra. one of the older and stranger items, and the one people giggle about.

cont. p.5 →
5 points

graha maitri

whether the rulers of the two moon signs are friends. genuinely one of the more sensible checks in the system, and the one worth arguing about.

cont. p.6 →
6 points

gana

deva, manushya, rakshasa. a nature grouping drawn from the nakshatras. it is a category, not a verdict on anybody's character.

cont. p.7 →
7 + 8 points

bhakoot and nadi carry 15 of the 36

both are all or nothing. so a couple can score well on six checks and still land under the line because of two. this is why so many matches collapse on a single row.

cont. p.8 →
the threshold

18 is a convention, not a law

it became the floor because it is half of 36, not because a single authoritative text put it there. treat it as a norm people agreed on, which is exactly what it is.
