someone tells u ur in saturn mahadasha and u nod. then they say "but it is the venus antardasha" and suddenly the reading changes tone completely.

that second planet is the antardasha, the sub-period inside the main period. if the mahadasha is the season of ur life, the antardasha is the month. it is shorter, it is more specific, and it is where most vedic timing actually happens.

the good news: it runs on one fixed order and one formula. u can work out every sub-period in ur life on the back of an envelope.

the short version

  • every mahadasha splits into nine antardashas, one per graha.
  • the first one always belongs to the mahadasha lord itself.
  • after that they follow the vimshottari order: ketu, venus, sun, moon, mars, rahu, jupiter, saturn, mercury.
  • length = mahadasha years × antardasha years ÷ 120.
  • they range from about 3.6 months (sun in sun) to 3 years 4 months (venus in venus).
  • astrologers read the pair: what each planet does in ur chart and how they get on.
  • bhukti is the same thing under another name.

what an antardasha is

An antardasha is a sub-period within a Vimshottari mahadasha. Each mahadasha is divided into nine antardashas, one ruled by each graha, in a fixed sequence and in proportion to each planet's own dasha years. The mahadasha lord sets the background theme; the antardasha lord brings its own significations to the foreground for a shorter stretch.

a useful picture: the mahadasha lord is the director of a film. every scene is shot in their style. the antardasha lord is the actor in the lead role for a particular stretch of scenes. same film, different lead, and the story moves differently.

the classical source is the brihat parashara hora shastra, which gives results for every mahadasha and antardasha combination. the logic is consistent across them: the result depends on both planets' strength, the houses they rule and occupy, and their relationship to each other.

the order they run in

Every mahadasha begins with its own lord's antardasha. The remaining eight follow the standard Vimshottari order of Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn and Mercury, continuing from wherever the mahadasha lord sits in that cycle and wrapping round to the start.

write the nine in a circle: ketu, venus, sun, moon, mars, rahu, jupiter, saturn, mercury. start at the mahadasha lord and go clockwise. that is ur antardasha order.

the one formula for length

Antardasha length equals the mahadasha planet's years multiplied by the antardasha planet's years, divided by 120. The 120 is the full Vimshottari cycle. So each planet takes the same share of every mahadasha that it takes of the whole 120 year cycle.

the dasha years, for reference: ketu 7, venus 20, sun 6, moon 10, mars 7, rahu 18, jupiter 16, saturn 19, mercury 17. they add up to 120.

so venus, with 20 of the 120 years, always gets one sixth of any mahadasha. inside a 19 year saturn mahadasha, one sixth is 3.17 years, about 3 years 2 months. inside a 6 year sun mahadasha, one sixth is exactly 1 year.

to turn decimal years into months, multiply the decimal by 12. 3.17 years is 3 years and 0.17 × 12, about 2 months.

tota says

the antardasha is not a new mood swing every few months. it is the same mahadasha story, told from a different seat. if ur in saturn-venus, it is still saturn's film. venus just has the lead for three years.

a full worked example

Saturn's mahadasha lasts 19 years and contains nine antardashas. Saturn-Saturn comes first at about 3 years, then Mercury, Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu and Jupiter. Each length is 19 times that planet's years divided by 120, and together they add back up to exactly 19 years.

these use the traditional 360 day dasha year that most software defaults to, so days can differ slightly between tools. the order and proportions never change.

one more thing: ur first mahadasha in life is partial. u are born partway through it, depending on how far the moon had travelled through its nakshatra. so ur first antardashas may already be "used up" at birth.

how the pair is read

Astrologers read the mahadasha and antardasha lords together. They look at what houses each planet rules and occupies in your chart, how strong each is, and how they relate: natural friendship, mutual placement from each other, and whether they aspect or conjoin. A supportive pair is read as smoother than a tense one.

the main questions, roughly in the order most astrologers ask them:

questionwhy it matters
which houses does the antardasha lord rule?those life areas come forward. a 10th lord antardasha leans toward career themes
where is it placed from the mahadasha lord?6th, 8th and 12th from each other is read as friction. trines and kendras as cooperation
are they natural friends?venus in saturn reads easier than sun in saturn, because sun and saturn are natural enemies
how strong is it?an exalted or debilitated antardasha lord delivers very differently
what are transits doing?gochar is read as the trigger inside the dasha's window

this is why two people in "saturn-venus" can have very different years. the label is the same. the houses, strength and relationships in their charts are not.

pratyantar and beyond

Each antardasha divides again into nine pratyantar dashas, using the same order and the same proportions. Pratyantars run from a few days to a few months. Some astrologers go further to sookshma and prana dashas, though those depend on a birth time accurate to the minute.

the deeper u go, the more ur birth time matters. a few minutes of error barely moves a mahadasha but can shift a pratyantar by days. this is where birth time accuracy stops being theoretical.

myths, one at a time

The antardasha matters more than the mahadasha.

The mahadasha sets the frame. The antardasha works inside it. They are read together, not ranked.

A Saturn or Rahu antardasha is always bad.

Results depend on what the planet rules, where it sits and how it relates to the mahadasha lord in your chart. No planet is uniformly bad.

Antardashas change on a single dramatic day.

The dates are exact, but themes tend to shift over weeks. Many astrologers treat the edges of a period as transitional.

Everyone born on the same day has the same antardasha.

The Moon moves about 13 degrees a day, so even hours apart can shift dasha dates by months.

faq

what is antardasha?

the sub-period inside a mahadasha. every mahadasha is divided into nine antardashas, one for each graha, in a fixed order. the mahadasha sets the overall theme and the antardasha lord colours a shorter stretch of it.

what is the difference between mahadasha and antardasha?

the mahadasha is the main period, from 6 to 20 years depending on the planet. the antardasha is a slice of it, from a few months to just over three years. astrologers read them as a pair.

how is antardasha length calculated?

multiply the mahadasha planet's years by the antardasha planet's years and divide by 120. so venus antardasha in saturn mahadasha is 19 times 20 divided by 120, about 3 years 2 months.

what is the order of antardashas?

the first antardasha belongs to the mahadasha lord itself. after that they follow the vimshottari order: ketu, venus, sun, moon, mars, rahu, jupiter, saturn, mercury, wrapping round until all nine have run.

what is bhukti?

another name for antardasha, common in south indian usage. the two words mean the same sub-period.

what is pratyantar dasha?

the third level down, a sub-period inside the antardasha, calculated with the same proportions. it can be a few days to a few months long, and some astrologers go further still to sookshma and prana dashas.

which antardasha is the most important?

there is no universal answer. it depends on what the antardasha planet owns and occupies in ur chart, and how it relates to the mahadasha lord. a friendly, well placed pair is usually read as smoother.

why is the first antardasha the same planet as the mahadasha?

because each mahadasha begins with its own lord's sub-period, sometimes written as saturn-saturn or venus-venus. it is the purest expression of that planet in the cycle.

how do i find my current antardasha?

u need ur birth date, time and place, so the moon's exact nakshatra position can be calculated. any vedic chart tool will list ur mahadasha, antardasha and their dates. ask tota shows them in the chart desk.

can antardasha change my life suddenly?

antardasha changes are where shifts are traditionally expected, because the planet in focus changes. in practice the change is usually a shift in themes over weeks rather than a switch flipped on a date.

what is the shortest antardasha?

sun antardasha inside sun mahadasha: 6 times 6 divided by 120, about 3 months 18 days.

what is the longest antardasha?

venus antardasha inside venus mahadasha: 20 times 20 divided by 120, exactly 3 years 4 months.
