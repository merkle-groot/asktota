# the ick, by venus sign

**twelve venus signs, twelve icks. 13.4s, loops.**
1080 x 1920, H.264 + AAC.

**Scheduled:** Friday 25 September 2026, 20:00 IST

The sequel to `13_moon-2am`, same engine, no new template. Twelve cards, one ick
each, then the dark panel: **comment ur venus sign**.

Venus rather than sun on purpose. The sun sign is something they already know, so
asking for it produces a comment and nothing else. Venus is a placement they have to
go and look up, and looking it up is an app open.

Every card is written the same way: the ick on the card, the chart read underneath.
The joke earns the second line, and the second line is the only reason this is not
just another dating meme account.

No voiceover. Sound design only, so a trending audio can go over the top inside
Instagram. Set the cover frame on the hook, not on frame 0.

Re-render: `python3 gencallout.py the-ick` from `.blogtools/social/`.
