the sun is the one placement everybody already knows, and it is the one most people misunderstand hardest.

it does not describe ur personality. it is formally a malefic. it rules exactly one sign, it is the only graha that damages the ones near it, and in indian practice it is weighted below both ur moon and ur lagna.

here is what surya actually carries, and what it is doing in each of the twelve houses.

the short version

  • the sun rules simha only. it and the moon are the only grahas with a single sign each.
  • it is a natural malefic. the word is krura, harsh, not evil. it burns what it touches.
  • it is the only graha that causes combustion, and the only one that can never suffer it.
  • it carries the atma, vitality, authority, the father, and anything official or governmental.
  • exalted in mesha at 10 degrees, debilitated in tula at 10 degrees. dasha of 6 years, the shortest run of any graha.
  • it is strongest in the 10th house by digbala, which is why it reads so strongly on career and standing.

what surya actually carries

The Sun signifies the atma, the self at its most essential, along with vitality, authority, the father, government, and anything formal or official. It is what a life is organised around, rather than how a person behaves day to day.

the useful distinction is between what u are built around and what u are like. the sun is the first of those. the moon and the lagna handle the second.

domainwhat the sun covers
The selfatma, the sense of being a distinct person with a direction
Vitalityphysical constitution, stamina, the heart, the eyes, the bones
Authoritycommand, position, being answerable and being answered to
The fatherpitru karaka, read alongside the 9th house
Governmentthe state, official bodies, licences, anything with a seal on it
Recognitionbeing seen, credited, named. not fame, which is a different thing

notice what is absent: emotion, mood, appetite, judgement. the sun does not do interior life. that belongs to the moon, and confusing the two is the single most common error in popular astrology.

why the sun is classed as a malefic

Classical Vedic astrology sorts the grahas into shubha (gentle) and krura (harsh). The Sun is krura, along with Mars, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu. Krura means harsh or cruel in effect, not morally bad, and the classification is about intensity rather than about outcomes.

this genuinely surprises people, because western popular astrology treats the sun as the warm centre of the chart.

the vedic reasoning is physical and consistent. the sun is hot, dry and unrelenting. anything it stands next to gets scorched. it does not soften, it does not accommodate, and it does not share a house comfortably.

which is why a sun on the lagna reads as someone who is impossible not to notice and slightly hard to be around, and why a sun sitting on a gentler graha is usually read as that graha being overwhelmed rather than supported.

the honest way to hold it: the sun is not unkind, it is uncompromising. those are different things and the tradition is describing the second.

the only graha that burns others

A graha within a certain distance of the Sun is called astangata, burnt, and is treated as having lost its independent voice. The Sun is the only graha that causes this, and the only one that can never experience it.

the mechanism is observational rather than mystical. a planet too close to the sun in the sky is invisible, lost in the glare. the tradition took that literally: a graha u cannot see is a graha that cannot speak for itself.

mesha and tula, and why those two

The Sun is exalted in Mesha at 10 degrees and debilitated in Tula at 10 degrees. Mesha is Mars's sign of immediate, unilateral action. Tula is Venus's sign of weighing, balancing and consulting others.

the pairing reads cleanly once u put the two signs side by side.

mesha asks nobody's permission. a graha whose entire business is being a distinct, self-directed centre is completely at home there. the self, in the sign of acting first.

tula is the opposite environment. tula weighs, compares and negotiates, and its whole competence is in accounting for the other person. a graha of unilateral selfhood is at its least comfortable in the sign of consultation.

and that is a much more useful reading than "debilitated is bad". a debilitated sun describes someone whose sense of self is built through other people rather than against them. it is a real pattern, it is extremely common, and it has cancellations. exalted and debilitated planets covers those.

ur sun sign is one position out of nine

The Sun moves about one degree a day, so a birth date alone fixes it. That is precisely why sun sign horoscopes can be written for a twelfth of the planet at a time, and why Indian practice has always used the Moon instead.

three things worth stacking up here.

  • it barely moves. one degree a day, thirty days per sign. everyone born within a month of u shares it.
  • indian families use the moon. when someone asks ur rashi, they mean ur moon sign. that convention is older than the sun sign horoscope by a very long way.
  • it is computed sidereally. so it is often not the sign u have been told. if u were born in roughly the first three weeks of ur western sign, ur vedic sun sign is the one before it. the arithmetic is here.

none of that makes the sun unimportant. it makes the sign of the sun a small part of what the sun is doing. the house it sits in, what it burns, and what it rules for ur lagna all matter more.

the sun in each of the twelve houses

The Sun in a house puts identity, authority and visibility into that department of life, and applies heat to it. Read each line as "this is where u need to be seen, and where u will not compromise".

2ndidentity tied to money, family standing and speech. a voice that carries.
3rdan upachaya house. courage that grows with use. self through effort and initiative.

the 6th and 11th are worth pausing on, because they contradict the reflex that malefics are bad news. those are upachaya houses, where harsh grahas are said to improve with time, and the sun does genuinely well in both.

the father question, handled carefully

Surya is the pitru karaka, the significator of the father, and is read alongside the 9th house. The responsible way to use that is to describe the condition of the graha, not to make claims about a real person's life or behaviour.

this is the placement most likely to be used badly, so it is worth being explicit.

a difficult sun does not mean a bad father. it commonly describes a person's relationship with authority, with being told what to do, and with the idea of a father figure, which is a different and much more defensible thing to say.

the honest reading is about the reader, because the reader is the only person whose chart u have. anything else is a claim about somebody who did not ask, based on a chart nobody cast.

and there is a technical point too: karaka and house often disagree, and when they do, the tradition does not have a tidy resolution. that disagreement is information, not a problem to be smoothed over.

myths, one at a time

Your sun sign is your personality.

It is what ur life is built around. Personality lives closer to the moon and the lagna, and in Vedic practice the moon is weighted far higher.

The Sun is a benefic because it is warm and central.

Vedic astrology classes it as krura, harsh. It is hot, dry and uncompromising, and it burns whatever sits close to it.

A debilitated Sun in Tula means low confidence.

It describes a self that is built through other people rather than against them. Common, cancellable, and not a defect.

My Vedic sun sign should match my western one.

Usually it will not. The two zodiacs start about 24 degrees apart, so most people move back a sign.

A strong Sun means fame.

It means recognition and authority in whichever house it sits. Fame is a different combination and involves far more than one graha.

The Sun in the 7th ruins marriage.

It is the graha least comfortable sharing a house, placed in the house of sharing. That is a tension to read, not a verdict, and the 7th lord and Venus matter more.

faq

what does the sun represent in vedic astrology?

the soul, the self, vitality, authority, the father, government and anything official. surya is called atmakaraka in the general sense, the significator of the atma. it is what u are built around rather than how u behave.

which sign does the sun rule?

simha, and only simha. the sun and the moon are the only grahas that rule a single sign each. its mooltrikona is simha from 0 to 20 degrees.

where is the sun exalted and debilitated?

exalted in mesha, deepest at 10 degrees, and debilitated in tula, deepest at 10 degrees. mesha is mars's sign of direct action and tula is venus's sign of weighing other people.

is the sun a benefic or a malefic?

a natural malefic, which surprises most people. the classical term is krura, meaning harsh rather than evil. the sun burns what it touches, and that is a description of intensity, not of morality.

what is combustion and why is the sun involved?

a graha too close to the sun loses its independent voice and is called astangata, burnt. the sun is the only graha that causes this and the only one that can never suffer it.

how long is sun mahadasha?

6 years, the shortest of the nine after nothing. it is often described as a short, sharp period concerned with recognition, authority and the father.

which nakshatras does the sun rule?

krittika, uttara phalguni and uttara ashadha. if ur moon was in one of those at birth, ur vimshottari dasha sequence started with the sun.

which house is the sun strongest in?

the 10th, by directional strength. digbala is the strength a graha gains from a particular direction, and the sun gets its full share in the 10th house of career and public standing.

does the sun sign matter in vedic astrology?

less than u have been told. it is one position out of nine, and indian practice weights the moon and the lagna far more heavily. it is also computed sidereally, so it is often not the sign u grew up calling urs.

what does the sun say about my father?

the tradition treats surya as the significator of the father, alongside the 9th house. read them together rather than separately, and read the condition of the graha rather than jumping to a conclusion about a real person.

is a debilitated sun in tula bad?

it is the placement where the self is least comfortable asserting itself alone, in the sign that weighs everybody else's view. it is common, cancellations apply, and plenty of very effective people have it.

what is the difference between the sun and the lagna?

the sun is what u are built around. the lagna is how u arrive and the frame every house is counted from. they answer different questions and the lagna is the more structural of the two.
