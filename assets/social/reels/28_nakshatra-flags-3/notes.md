## what to do with it afterwards

Three obvious continuations, in order of how cheap they are:

1. **The comment archive.** The same trick `NEXT_UP.md` proposes for the 2am
   reel. Build a reel out of the funniest nakshatra replies, credited by first
   name, one card each. It rewards commenters and teaches the audience that
   commenting gets them on the account.
2. **Nakshatra as the friend in the group chat.** Same twenty seven, different
   frame. Four reels of seven or the nine most common.
3. **Pada level.** Each nakshatra splits into four padas. That is 108 cards and a
   genuinely bottomless format, but it needs a birth time to be useful, so it
   belongs after an install rather than before one.
