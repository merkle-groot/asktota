the twelve houses, explained one at a time

signs tell u how a planet behaves. houses tell u where in ur life it does it. here is what each of the twelve bhavas is actually responsible for, and why five of ur houses being empty is completely normal.

JULY 29, 2026 · 15 MIN READ

the houses are the part of a chart that does the specific work, and the part most beginners skip because signs are easier to google.

a planet's sign tells u its manner. its house tells u its address. Saturn in Makara is a description of temperament. Saturn in the 7th house is a description of ur marriage. one of those is far more useful than the other when u are trying to understand ur own life, and this piece walks all twelve, with the calculator so u can see where ur own grahas actually landed.

the short version

  • the twelve houses, or bhavas, split ur chart into twelve areas of life.
  • house 1 begins at ur lagna, ur rising sign. the remaining eleven follow in zodiac order from there.
  • Vedic astrology almost always uses whole sign houses: one sign equals one house, no cusps, no arguments.
  • houses come in families. kendras (1, 4, 7, 10) are the structural pillars. trikonas (1, 5, 9) are the fortunate ones. dusthanas (6, 8, 12) are the difficult ones.
  • an empty house is not an empty life. read it through its ruling planet instead.
  • the two houses people ask about most are the 7th, for marriage, and the 10th, for career.

what a house actually is

A house is a twelfth of the sky as seen from ur birthplace, measured from the eastern horizon. The sign that was rising becomes ur 1st house, and the next eleven signs become houses 2 through 12 in order. Each one governs a specific department of life.

signs are a division of the zodiac. houses are a division of ur own local sky. that is the difference, and it is why houses need ur birth time and place while signs mostly do not.

think of it as two overlapping wheels. the zodiac wheel is fixed and shared: everyone born on the same day has the sun in the same sign. the house wheel is personal and spins once every twenty-four hours as the earth turns. ur birth minute stops that spin at one particular angle, and that angle is ur lagna.

which is why two people born on the same date in the same city, four hours apart, share almost every planetary sign and share almost no house placements. same sky, different rooms.

whole sign houses, and why the argument does not apply here

In whole sign houses, the entire rising sign is the 1st house, the entire next sign is the 2nd, and so on. A planet's house is decided by its sign alone. Western astrology's long argument about Placidus versus Koch versus equal houses mostly does not touch Vedic practice.

western astrology has a genuine and unresolved fight about how to divide houses, because most of its systems split the sky unevenly by time or space, which produces houses of different sizes and planets that fall on cusps.

Vedic practice sidesteps the whole thing. one sign, one house, thirty degrees each, no exceptions. if ur lagna is Kumbha, then the whole of Kumbha is ur 1st house even if the ascendant point falls at 29 degrees, and Meena is entirely ur 2nd.

the practical benefit is that a planet cannot be ambiguous about which house it is in. the practical cost is that a planet at 29 degrees of ur 1st house sits very close to ur 2nd house in real sky terms while being firmly counted in the 1st. some practitioners handle that with bhava chalit, an additional chart that adjusts for it. most read the whole sign chart as primary and treat chalit as a cross-check.

the house families: kendra, trikona, dusthana

Houses group into families that share a character. Kendras (1, 4, 7, 10) hold structure and visibility. Trikonas (1, 5, 9) hold fortune and dharma. Dusthanas (6, 8, 12) hold friction and loss. Upachayas (3, 6, 10, 11) improve with time and effort.

the 1st house appearing in both the kendra and trikona lists is not a filing error. it is the only house that is both, which is why the tradition treats it as the strongest point in a chart.

a note on the dusthanas, since they get oversold as disaster zones: the 6th is also the house of competitive work, service and the ability to beat a problem. the 8th is the house of research, inheritance and genuine transformation. the 12th is the house of foreign lands, sleep, retreat and release. plenty of successful lives have planets there, and plenty of unremarkable ones do not.

all twelve houses, one at a time

Each house rules a cluster of connected topics. The 1st is body and self, the 2nd money and speech, the 3rd courage and siblings, the 4th home and mother, the 5th creativity and children, the 6th work and health, the 7th marriage, the 8th upheaval, the 9th luck and belief, the 10th career, the 11th income, the 12th release.

1st house: tanu bhava

the body, the temperament, the way u enter a room. it is the reference point for the whole chart, so its condition colours everything. planets here are the most visible ones in ur life because they sit on ur own front door. ruler of this house is ur lagna lord, the closest thing the chart has to a main character.

2nd house: dhana bhava

money u earn and hold, the family u came from, food, and speech. the speech connection surprises people until u notice the house also covers the face and mouth. a strong 2nd tends to read as resourcefulness rather than inherited wealth, which is a 4th and 8th house theme.

3rd house: sahaja bhava

courage, initiative, younger siblings, short journeys, hands and communication. this is the house of ur own effort, distinct from luck. writers, salespeople and anyone who succeeds by sheer persistence usually have something going on here. it is also an upachaya, so it strengthens as u age.

4th house: bandhu bhava

home, the mother, land and vehicles, and ur emotional baseline. it sits at the very bottom of the chart, which is the tradition being literal: this is the floor. schooling up to a point also lives here, as does the feeling of having somewhere to return to.

5th house: putra bhava

creativity, romance, children, speculation, and the kind of learning u do because u want to. it is a trikona, so it carries good fortune. the mix of children, romance and speculation is not random. all three are things u put out into the world without being able to control the result.

6th house: ari bhava

daily work, routine, health, debt, litigation, and enemies. the least glamorous house and one of the most practical. it is where u solve problems for a living. a well-occupied 6th often shows up in people who are unusually good at grinding through obstacles, which is why it is also an upachaya.

7th house: yuvati bhava

marriage, business partnership, and open opposition. anyone u face across a table. it sits exactly opposite the 1st, which is the tradition making a point: the partner is the counterweight to the self. this is the house every matchmaking conversation is really about.

8th house: randhra bhava

upheaval, secrets, inheritance, other people's money, research, and things that end so something else can start. it has the worst reputation and the most interesting contents. surgeons, investigators, researchers and psychologists frequently have activity here. it also rules joint finances, insurance and everything to do with what u receive rather than earn.

9th house: dharma bhava

luck, belief, teachers, higher learning, long journeys, and the father. classically the most fortunate house in the chart. it is where ur sense of what is worth doing comes from, and it pairs with the 3rd, which is where ur effort comes from. belief and effort, sitting opposite each other.

10th house: karma bhava

career, public reputation, and the thing u are known for. the highest point of the chart, which is again literal: this is what people can see from a distance. worth separating from the 6th, which is the work u do. the 10th is the standing u get for doing it.

11th house: labha bhava

income, gains, networks, older siblings, and the things u want. distinct from the 2nd, which is money u hold. the 11th is money that arrives, especially through other people. it is the house of the group, the circle, and the ambition that pulls u forward.

12th house: vyaya bhava

expenditure, sleep, foreign lands, isolation, hospitals, spiritual practice, and letting go. the most misread house in the chart. yes it covers loss, but it covers loss in the sense of release, including the release of things worth releasing. it is also the standard house for foreign residence, which is why so many people who move abroad have something here.

why empty houses are normal and mean nothing

Nine grahas cannot occupy twelve houses, so every chart has empty ones. Five to seven is typical. An empty house is read through its lord: find the planet that rules the sign on that house and see where it sits and how it is doing.

this is the most common source of unnecessary worry we see. someone looks at their chart, sees nothing in the 7th, and concludes they will never marry.

the arithmetic alone should settle it. nine grahas, twelve houses, and grahas cluster: they are not evenly distributed, so several often share one house, which leaves even more houses empty. a chart with only three empty houses is unusual, not fortunate.

the technique for reading an empty house is straightforward:

  1. find the sign sitting on that house.
  2. find that sign's ruling planet. that planet is the house lord.
  3. find where the lord is sitting, what condition it is in, and what else touches it.

so an empty 7th house in Simha means ur 7th lord is the Sun. if that Sun is strong and well placed, ur 7th house is being looked after from wherever it stands. the house is not empty in any meaningful sense. its manager is just working from a different office.

how to actually read a house

To read any house, check four things in order: which sign is on it, which planets sit in it, where its lord is placed, and which planets aspect it. Any conclusion drawn from only one of those four is guesswork wearing confidence.

  1. the sign on the house. this sets the manner. a 10th house in Vrishchika behaves nothing like a 10th house in Tula.
  2. the occupants. planets sitting in the house act on it directly and loudly. more than one and they interact.
  3. the lord and its placement. often more important than the occupants, and almost always more important than beginners expect. a house whose lord is strong is a house being managed well.
  4. aspects. in Vedic astrology every graha aspects the 7th house from itself. Mars additionally aspects the 4th and 8th, Jupiter the 5th and 9th, and Saturn the 3rd and 10th. these are whole-house aspects, not degree-based ones, which is another structural difference from western practice.

the fourth point is the one that catches people out. ur 7th house is aspected by every single planet sitting in ur 1st, always, by definition. so a chart with a crowded 1st house has a busy 7th whether or not anything is standing in it.

what gets repeated about houses, and what is actually the case

An empty 7th house means I will not get married.

Most charts have five to seven empty houses and the 7th is frequently one of them. The house is read through its lord, which is sitting somewhere doing its job.

The 6th, 8th and 12th houses are bad and planets there are wasted.

They are difficult, not wasted. The 6th produces people who are excellent at solving problems, the 8th produces researchers and investigators, and the 12th is the standard house for living abroad and for genuine spiritual practice.

A planet in its own sign in any house is automatically good.

Dignity and house placement are separate variables. A comfortable planet running a difficult house is a mixed picture, and reading either one alone gives you half a sentence.

Houses are the same in Vedic and western astrology.

The topics overlap heavily, but the division method usually does not. Vedic practice uses whole sign houses. Most western practice uses quadrant systems that produce houses of unequal size.

A crowded house means that area of life dominates everything.

It means that area is busy and complicated, which is not the same as dominant. Crowding often reads as several competing pressures in one department rather than one loud success.

mistakes worth not making

  • counting houses from ur sun sign. houses are counted from the lagna. counting from the moon is a legitimate secondary technique. counting from the sun is a western import that does not belong in a Vedic reading.
  • ignoring the house lord. occupants are visible and lords are structural. reading only occupants is like judging a company by who happens to be in the lobby.
  • forgetting the aspects. a house with nothing in it can still be under heavy pressure from a planet aspecting it from across the chart.
  • treating the 8th and 12th as verdicts. they are the two most misread houses in the system and the two that most reliably get used to frighten people.
  • confusing the 6th with the 10th. the 6th is the work u do. the 10th is what u are known for. plenty of people have a strong 6th and an unremarkable 10th, which reads as competence without recognition.

the house that gets people worried is almost never the one worth worrying about. an empty 7th sends people into a spiral while a badly placed 7th lord goes completely unnoticed. always ask where the manager is before u panic about the empty office.

the receipt

the house significations here follow Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, the standard reference for bhava meanings and lordships, cross-checked against Phaladeepika for the kendra, trikona and dusthana groupings. the aspect rules given, every graha aspecting the 7th, Mars additionally the 4th and 8th, Jupiter the 5th and 9th, Saturn the 3rd and 10th, are the classical special aspects from the same source.

the whole sign house system used in the calculator is the standard in Vedic practice and the oldest house system in continuous use. we have not applied bhava chalit adjustments, which some practitioners prefer, and we say so rather than presenting one convention as the only one.

house meanings are traditional interpretation, not measurement. the sign and planet positions the calculator produces are measurement, and those are checkable against any ephemeris.

frequently asked questions

what are the 12 houses in Vedic astrology?

twelve divisions of ur local sky, counted from ur rising sign, each governing a department of life: body, wealth, courage, home, creativity, work, marriage, upheaval, fortune, career, income, and release.

how do I find my houses?

u need ur birth date, time and place. the rising sign becomes house 1 and the following signs become houses 2 to 12 in order. the calculator on this page will do it.

which house is for marriage?

the 7th. it covers marriage, business partnership and open opposition, which is the tradition being honest about what partnership involves.

which house is for career?

the 10th, for career and public standing. the 6th covers the daily work itself and the 11th covers income from it, so career questions usually read across all three.

which house is for money?

the 2nd for money u earn and hold, and the 11th for money that comes in, especially through networks and other people. the 8th covers money that is not yours, such as inheritance and joint finances.

what does an empty house mean?

nothing on its own. nine grahas cannot fill twelve houses, so five to seven empty houses is normal. read the house through its ruling planet instead.

what is a house lord?

the planet that rules the sign sitting on that house. its condition and placement tell u how that department of life is being managed.

what are kendra houses?

1, 4, 7 and 10, the angular houses. they are the structural pillars of a chart and planets there tend to be prominent in a life.

what are trikona houses?

1, 5 and 9, the trines. classically the most fortunate houses in the chart.

what are dusthana houses?

6, 8 and 12, associated with friction, upheaval and loss. difficult, but not empty of value: they also cover competitive work, research and foreign residence.

which house is the strongest?

the 1st, because it is the only house that is both a kendra and a trikona. after that it depends entirely on the individual chart.

why does Vedic astrology use whole sign houses?

because it is the oldest system in continuous use and because it removes cusp ambiguity entirely. one sign is one house, so a planet's house is never in dispute.

do houses change during my life?

no. the house structure is fixed at birth. what changes is which planets are transiting through them and which dasha period u are running.

which house shows foreign settlement?

the 12th is the classical house of foreign lands, usually read alongside the 9th for long journeys and the 4th for whether ur roots hold u in place.

what house is health in?

the 6th for illness and daily health, the 1st for overall vitality, and the 8th for chronic or sudden conditions. we would add that no chart is a diagnosis and none of this replaces a doctor.

the five things worth remembering

  1. houses are the division of ur local sky, which is why they need ur birth time and place while signs mostly do not.
  2. house 1 begins at ur lagna. everything else follows from that one placement being correct.
  3. Vedic practice uses whole sign houses, so a planet's house is never ambiguous.
  4. an empty house is read through its lord. five to seven empty houses is the normal state of a chart.
  5. read four things per house, in order: the sign on it, the planets in it, where its lord went, and what aspects it.
