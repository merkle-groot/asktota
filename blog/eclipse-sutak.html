what an eclipse is, astronomically

a solar eclipse is the moon getting between us and the sun, dropping its shadow on part of the earth.

a lunar eclipse is the earth getting between the sun and the moon, so the moon walks through our shadow.

both need a very particular line-up. the moon's path around us is tilted, so most months it passes above or below the sun instead of across it. an eclipse only happens near the two spots where those two paths actually cross.

those two crossing spots matter a lot here. they have names, and in vedic astrology they count as planets.

rahu and ketu are the crossing points

astronomers call them the lunar nodes. vedic astrology calls them Rahu and Ketu, and counts them as two of the nine grahas, right alongside the sun, the moon and the five planets u can see.

they are not made of anything. no rock, no surface, nothing to land on. they are just the two places where the moon's path crosses the sun's path in our sky.

an eclipse can only happen when the sun or moon is sitting near one of those two spots. that is why eclipses come in pairs about two weeks apart, roughly twice a year.

so calling Rahu and Ketu "shadow planets" is not poetry. it is a description. they are the shape in the sky that lets a shadow fall in the right place.

what an eclipse actually activates

an eclipse does not light up ur whole chart. it lights up one spot: the exact rashi and nakshatra where it happens.

if u have a planet sitting on or near that spot, that planet gets poked. good or bad depends on which planet it is and what it looks after for u.

if u have nothing there, the eclipse is weather. real, happening, just not addressed to u.

so "go check ur chart" is the honest answer to "will this eclipse hit me". "eclipses are dangerous, cancel everything" is not. it is a guess wearing the costume of a rule.

sutak, and why visibility is the whole rule

sutak kala is the stretch of time before and during an eclipse when people hold off. no cooking, no eating, nothing new started. temples shut. some people stay indoors. it is old, real and widely kept.

and it is tied to seeing the eclipse.

the classical reasoning is simple. sutak is a response to something happening in ur sky. if the eclipse is under ur horizon, on the far side of the planet, there is nothing there to respond to. that is not a modern dodge. it sits in the same texts that describe sutak.

so the rule has two halves, and most posts only give u the first. an eclipse has to be happening, and u have to be able to see it.

so does the eclipse do nothing, then

no, and this is the bit that always gets mashed together.

the eclipse itself happens at one moment for the whole planet. the Rahu and Ketu activation of that rashi and nakshatra is real everywhere, whether anyone is awake to watch it or not. that part is maths, not eyesight.

sutak is a separate layer sitting on top: a practice built around witnessing it. so ur chart can be touched by an eclipse u cannot see. u just do not keep sutak for one u cannot see.

pulling those two apart, the astronomy and the observance, is the whole point of this post.

faq

is the august 12 solar eclipse visible from india?

no. it peaks at 11:17pm IST, long after sunset here. the sun is not in the sky, so there is nothing to see.

is the august 27-28 lunar eclipse visible from india?

no. it peaks around 9:42am IST the next morning, by which point the moon has already set.

if an eclipse isn't visible from india, does sutak still apply?

no. sutak is tied to being able to watch the eclipse. if u cannot see it from where u are, the observance does not apply.

what are rahu and ketu?

the two spots where the moon's path crosses the sun's path in our sky. vedic astrology counts them as grahas. they are not made of anything, they are just the line-up that makes eclipses possible.

does an eclipse affect me if it isn't visible where I live?

the eclipse and its effect on ur chart happen either way. the part that does not apply is sutak, because that one is about watching it.

two eclipses, no sutak, and an answer that depends on ur own chart either way. whether one of them lands on something of urs is a question about placements, not headlines. pull up ur rashi and look.
