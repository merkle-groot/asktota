two babies, same parents, same hospital, born eight minutes apart. one becomes a surgeon, the other a drummer. so what was the chart doing?

the twins question is the oldest objection to astrology, older than science. jyotish has had centuries to answer it, and its answer is specific: a few minutes barely move the planets, but they can move the fine divisional charts and the dasha dates. that is where the tradition says twins split.

what twins share

Twins born minutes apart share nearly the whole main chart: the same planets in the same signs, almost always the same houses, and usually the same ascendant sign. The Sun moves about one degree a day and even the Moon only about half a degree an hour, so a short gap barely moves them.

this is not a weakness people discovered recently. classical astrologers knew perfectly well that twins exist, and that their main charts look the same. the whole apparatus of finer divisions is partly the tradition's response.

so if someone reads twins from the d1 alone and tells them different things, they are reading the people, not the charts.

what a few minutes changes

A few minutes changes the exact ascendant degree, which can shift the ascendant in fine divisional charts. The D9 ascendant changes roughly every 13 minutes, the D10 every 12 and the D60 every 2. Dasha dates shift slightly. If the gap is long, or the ascendant sits near a sign boundary, even the main chart can differ.

how the tradition separates twins

Vedic astrologers separate twins mainly through divisional charts, especially the D60 shashtiamsa, which Parashara weights very highly and which changes every couple of minutes. Some practitioners also use special techniques for the second twin, such as reading from a rotated chart. These methods exist because the main chart alone does not separate twins.

the d60 is the tradition's finest standard chart, dividing each sign into sixty slices of half a degree. parashara gives it more weight than any other varga in his strength scoring. for twins, it is often the first chart that actually differs.

one obvious catch: the d60 is only meaningful if both birth times are accurate to a minute or two. hospital records are often rounded, so many twin readings are asking the finest tool to work with the roughest input. see why ur birth time matters.

what the research found

Geoffrey Dean and Ivan Kelly studied 2,101 people born in London in early March 1958, many within minutes of each other. Comparing these time twins on over 100 traits from childhood to adulthood, they found no more similarity between people born close together than between random pairs.

this is the scientific version of the twins question, and it is a fair one: if charts shaped personality, people with nearly identical charts should resemble each other more than chance. they did not. this fits the wider body of tests summarised in how accurate is vedic astrology.

a vedic astrologer might reply that the d60 and other fine charts differ between time twins too. that is true, and it also means the chart's effect, if any, would have to live in divisions most people's birth times cannot resolve.

everything that is not the chart

Even identical twins differ in experiences, relationships, choices, birth order and how others treat them. Fraternal twins also differ genetically. Classical Vedic texts themselves describe life as shaped by karma and effort together. So different lives from near-identical charts are expected whichever way you read astrology.

twin research in psychology finds that identical twins raised together still develop distinct personalities, partly because they actively differentiate from each other. one twin becomes "the outgoing one", the other "the careful one", and those roles shape choices for decades.

the tradition is comfortable with this. jyotish has always paired destiny with action. more in free will vs fate.

if u are a twin

Get both birth times recorded as precisely as possible, ideally from hospital records. Have each chart calculated separately. Treat the shared main chart as common ground and the divisional charts and dasha dates as where your readings diverge, and use both as prompts for reflection rather than predictions.

faq

do twins have the same birth chart?

nearly. twins born minutes apart share the same planets in the same signs and usually the same lagna sign. what differs is the exact lagna degree, the divisional charts, and slightly the dasha dates. how much depends on how far apart they were born.

why do twins have different lives if their charts are the same?

vedic astrology points to differences in the fine divisional charts and dasha timing. beyond astrology, twins differ in genes (if fraternal), experiences, choices and relationships. research on time twins found no extra similarity from shared charts.

how much does a few minutes change a birth chart?

the lagna moves about a degree every four minutes. the planets barely move: even the moon shifts only about one degree every two hours. so the d1 chart is usually identical, while the d9 lagna can change about every 13 minutes and the d60 every 2.

can two people have the same birth chart?

two people born at the same minute in the same place have the same chart. this happens constantly across a large population. those time twins do not lead identical lives, which is one of the most cited observations about astrology.

what does vedic astrology say about twins?

traditional astrologers use the divisional charts, especially the d60 shashtiamsa, and sometimes treat the second twin as reading from a rotated chart. these methods exist precisely because the d1 alone does not separate twins.

what is the d60 chart and why does it matter for twins?

the shashtiamsa divides each sign into 60 parts of half a degree. its lagna changes about every two minutes, so twins born a few minutes apart often have different d60 charts. parashara gives the d60 great weight.

what did the time twins study find?

geoffrey dean and ivan kelly followed 2,101 people born in london in march 1958 and compared those born close together on over 100 traits. they found no more similarity than between random pairs.

do twins have the same dasha?

the same dasha sequence and almost the same dates. the moon moves only about half a degree an hour, so a few minutes changes dasha dates by days or weeks, not years.

do twins born on different days have different charts?

yes. twins born either side of midnight can have a different moon nakshatra and sometimes a different moon sign, which changes their dasha timeline significantly.

are identical twins a problem for astrology?

they are one of the strongest tests of it, because they share genes, parents, upbringing and nearly the same chart, yet live distinct lives. astrology answers through fine divisional charts; critics argue this shows the chart is not the deciding factor.

which twin is older in astrology?

the one born first, with the earlier birth time. their charts are calculated separately from their exact times, so it matters to record both births to the minute.

should twins get separate readings?

yes, with their individual birth times recorded as precisely as possible. a reading that uses the same chart for both is skipping the part where twins actually differ.
