## a saturday series, on purpose

No. 23 ran the first twelve on Saturday 3 October. This runs eight more a week
later and no. 28 closes with the last seven on 17 October. Same slot, three
weeks running, and the CTA of each one names how many are left.

That turns a format into an appointment. A viewer whose nakshatra was not in the
first reel has a reason to come back, and the account gets three posts out of one
idea with nothing repeated.

## why eight and not twelve

Twelve was the ceiling in the first reel because twelve cards at 24 frames is
already past ten seconds. Splitting the remaining fifteen as eight and seven
keeps both reels under the line instead of stretching one to fifteen and losing
the back half of the audience.

## the correctness thing

Every card's `when` line names the ruling graha and every `sub` line has to agree
with it. The first reel shipped with mrigashira saying "budha in seek mode" under
a `when` of "mangal rules it", which is simply wrong and would have been the top
comment. Fixed and re-rendered on 21 September.

Check the rulerships against the standard list before adding cards. It is the one
kind of error this format cannot absorb, because the whole pitch is that jyotish
has a layer western astrology does not.
