"mahapurusha" means great person. it is a big word to find in ur own chart, and it is one of the cleaner rules in jyotish.

the panch mahapurusha yogas are five combinations, one for each of the five planets that can own and be exalted in signs: mars, mercury, jupiter, venus and saturn. the rule is the same for all five: the planet sits in its own sign or exaltation sign, in a kendra from the lagna. that is a planet at full strength in a strong position.

the short version

  • the rule: own or exaltation sign + kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) from the lagna.
  • ruchaka (mars), bhadra (mercury), hamsa (jupiter), malavya (venus), shasha (saturn).
  • sun, moon, rahu and ketu are not part of it.
  • each yoga turns up in roughly one chart in twelve; about a third of charts have at least one.
  • combustion, affliction and a weak lagna lord reduce it.
  • results come mainly in the dasha of the planet.

the one rule behind all five

A Panch Mahapurusha yoga forms when Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn occupies its own sign or its sign of exaltation, and that sign falls in a kendra, the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th house, from the ascendant. Each planet produces its own named yoga.

two strengths stacked together. dignity: the planet is at home or at its peak, so it can express itself fully. angularity: kendras are the pillars of the chart, the most visible and active houses. a dignified planet on a pillar is the tradition's picture of a planet that shapes the whole life.

see exalted and debilitated planets for dignity, and the 12 houses for why kendras matter.

the five yogas

Ruchaka is Mars in Aries, Scorpio or Capricorn; Bhadra is Mercury in Gemini or Virgo; Hamsa is Jupiter in Sagittarius, Pisces or Cancer; Malavya is Venus in Taurus, Libra or Pisces; and Shasha is Saturn in Capricorn, Aquarius or Libra, each in a kendra from the ascendant.

the names are lovely. hamsa is the swan, a symbol of discernment. malavya refers to the malwa region, linked in old texts with prosperity. shasha is often read as hare or as related to rulership. ruchaka suggests radiance. bhadra means auspicious or gentle.

how common they are

Each planet is in one of its qualifying signs roughly a quarter of the time (Mercury about a sixth), and in a kendra about a third of the time, so each yoga appears in roughly one chart in twelve to eighteen. Combined, roughly a third of all charts have at least one Mahapurusha yoga on the basic rule.

so "u have a mahapurusha yoga" is good to hear and not rare. what lifts one from common to significant is in the next section, and it is the same story as with gajakesari yoga: presence is the start, strength is the reading.

what makes one count

A Mahapurusha yoga is stronger when the planet is free from combustion and malefic affliction, not in the first or last degree of the sign, supported by a strong ascendant lord, and well placed in the navamsa. Some texts say it weakens when the planet is conjunct the Sun or Moon.

combustion matters most for bhadra and malavya. mercury and venus are always close to the sun, so a bhadra yoga with mercury three degrees from the sun is a much quieter thing than the label suggests.

which ascendants can have which

Because the qualifying signs must fall in a kendra, each yoga is possible only for certain ascendants. For example, Hamsa is possible for Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn ascendants (Cancer in a kendra) and for Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces ascendants (Sagittarius or Pisces in a kendra).

a quick way to think about it: movable-sign ascendants (aries, cancer, libra, capricorn) have the other movable signs in their kendras. fixed-sign ascendants have fixed signs there. dual-sign ascendants have dual signs. so:

so bhadra yoga is only possible for dual-sign ascendants, and only fixed-sign ascendants cannot have hamsa. small facts like these make "u have all five" claims easy to check.

check ur chart

Find your ascendant and list the signs in your 1st, 4th, 7th and 10th houses. Check whether Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus or Saturn sits in one of those signs and that sign is its own or exaltation sign. If so, you have that planet's Mahapurusha yoga; then check combustion and affliction.

  1. find ur lagna.
  2. list ur four kendra signs.
  3. check each of the five planets against the table above.
  4. check combustion, especially for mercury and venus.
  5. note the planet's dasha in ur timeline.

myths, one at a time

Mahapurusha yogas are extremely rare.

About a third of charts have at least one on the basic rule. Strong, unafflicted ones are rarer.

A Mahapurusha yoga guarantees greatness.

It describes a strong, prominent planet. Results depend on strength, the whole chart and the dashas.

The Sun and Moon can form Mahapurusha yogas.

Only Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn form them.

Anyone can have all five.

No ascendant can host more than four of the five, and each yoga needs its planet in a specific kendra sign.

faq

what is panch mahapurusha yoga?

a set of five yogas formed when mars, mercury, jupiter, venus or saturn occupies its own sign or exaltation sign in a kendra, the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th house. mahapurusha means great person.

what are the five mahapurusha yogas?

ruchaka (mars), bhadra (mercury), hamsa (jupiter), malavya (venus) and shasha (saturn).

how is ruchaka yoga formed?

mars in aries or scorpio, its own signs, or capricorn, its exaltation, in a kendra from the lagna. it is traditionally linked to courage, leadership and physical strength.

how is bhadra yoga formed?

mercury in gemini or virgo, which is both its own and exaltation sign, in a kendra. it is linked to intelligence, eloquence and skill in communication and business.

how is hamsa yoga formed?

jupiter in sagittarius or pisces, its own signs, or cancer, its exaltation, in a kendra. it is linked to wisdom, ethics, respect and a teacher's nature.

how is malavya yoga formed?

venus in taurus or libra, its own signs, or pisces, its exaltation, in a kendra. it is linked to charm, artistic taste, comfort and harmonious relationships.

how is shasha yoga formed?

saturn in capricorn or aquarius, its own signs, or libra, its exaltation, in a kendra. it is linked to authority, discipline, endurance and influence over groups or institutions.

how common is panch mahapurusha yoga?

each individual yoga appears in roughly one chart in twelve to eighteen. taken together, roughly a third of charts have at least one of the five. strength conditions decide how much each matters.

are sun and moon part of mahapurusha yoga?

no. the five yogas use only the five 'star planets', mars, mercury, jupiter, venus and saturn. the sun and moon and the nodes are excluded.

is mahapurusha yoga counted from the moon too?

the classical definition counts kendras from the lagna. some astrologers also accept kendras from the moon as a weaker form.

what weakens a mahapurusha yoga?

combustion, heavy affliction by malefics, the planet being in a very early or late degree of the sign, or a weak lagna lord. some texts also say the yoga gives less if the planet is with the sun or moon.

when does a mahapurusha yoga give results?

most clearly in the dasha or antardasha of the planet forming it. as with all yogas, the chart shows the potential and the dasha shows the timing.
