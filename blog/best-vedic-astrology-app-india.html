how to pick a vedic astrology app, from people who make one

we build ask tota, so treat this as a pitch with its working shown. here are the ten checks that separate a real jyotish engine from a horoscope generator, the three completely different products all calling themselves astrology apps, and the places our competitors are straightforwardly better.

three products, one word

Almost every "best astrology app" list compares things that are not competitors. There are three distinct products: marketplaces that sell you minutes with a human astrologer, computation tools that draw charts and run calculations, and AI companions that generate readings from your chart.

categorywhat u are buyingbest whenthe catch
Consultation marketplaceminutes with a personu want a human to answer a specific life questionper-minute pricing rewards long calls, and quality varies enormously between practitioners
Computation toolcharts, vargas, dashas, ashtakavargau are learning jyotish or already know itit will not interpret anything for u. that is the point
AI companionreadings generated from ur own chartu want ur actual chart explained in plain languageit is a language model over real calculations, and it should say so

the mistake is picking the wrong category and then being disappointed by a product doing exactly its job. a computation tool that refuses to tell u what anything means is not broken. a marketplace that costs money per minute is not a scam. they are different things.

ask tota is the third kind. it computes a real chart and then talks about it. if what u want is a person on a call, u want the first kind, and we will not be the answer.

the ten checks

These are ordered by how much they narrow the field. The first two eliminate most apps on their own.

  1. which ayanamsa? lahiri is the indian standard. an app that will not tell u has either not decided or is not sidereal. this single question removes most of the list.
  2. is it genuinely sidereal? run the ten second test in the next section. a surprising number of apps labelled vedic serve western positions.
  3. does it name its ephemeris? swiss ephemeris is the usual answer. an app that names nothing usually has nothing to name.
  4. can u see the degrees? every graha, to the minute, with its nakshatra and pada. if u cannot see the numbers, u cannot check anything.
  5. which house system? whole sign is standard in vedic practice. if it is using placidus without saying so, u are looking at a western chart in sanskrit clothing.
  6. how does it handle no birth time? the honest answer is to give u sun, moon, nakshatra and dasha and tell u the houses are unavailable. silently substituting noon is the tell.
  7. does it show dasha dates? mahadasha and antardasha with start and end dates. timing is where vedic astrology actually lives, and an app without it is a horoscope generator.
  8. does it sell remedies? and if so, does it earn on them. an app that diagnoses a dosha and then routes u to a gemstone it takes commission on has a conflict of interest.
  9. what happens to ur birth data? who processes it, where, and can u delete it from inside the app. under the DPDP Act u are entitled to ask and to be told.
  10. is the pricing legible? what is free, what is paid, and what the limits are, visible before u pay rather than after.

if u only run two of these, run one and six. the ayanamsa question tells u whether the maths is vedic. the birth time question tells u whether the app is honest when it does not know something.

tota says

the fastest way to judge any astrology app: find out what it does when it does not know ur birth time. an app that says "we can't give u houses without it" is being straight with u. one that hands u a confident lagna anyway is guessing and hiding it.

the sidereal test u can run in ten seconds

Enter a birth date in the middle of August and look at the sun sign the app returns. Tropical astrology calls that Leo. Sidereal astrology calls most of August Karka. If a 15 August birthday comes back as Leo, the app is not sidereal, whatever it says on the listing.

this works because the two zodiacs have drifted about 24 degrees apart, which is most of a sign. if u were born in roughly the first three weeks of ur western sign, ur vedic sun sign is the one before it.

so anything that returns the western answer while calling itself vedic has either not implemented an ayanamsa or has left it switched off. why ur sun sign is different in vedic astrology has the full arithmetic.

a second, harder test if u want one: check whether the app's moon sign matches what ur family has always called ur rashi. indian families use the moon sign, and it is computed sidereally, so a mismatch there is a strong signal something is off.

red flags

The reliable warning signs are commerce patterns rather than astrological ones. Urgency, fear followed immediately by a price, and readings that cannot be checked.

  • a countdown timer on a remedy. planetary positions do not expire at midnight. sale prices do.
  • a dosha diagnosis followed by a purchase link. the order matters. explanation first, then price, or it is a funnel.
  • predictions with dates. the instruments run in months and years. an app promising a marriage month is exceeding what the technique supports. see when will i get married.
  • no way to see the chart. prose with no numbers behind it cannot be verified, and anything unverifiable is entertainment.
  • readings that never say "we do not know". a system that is always confident is not measuring anything.
  • no delete option. ur birth details are personal data. if u cannot remove them, ask why not.

myths, one at a time

The paid app must be more accurate.

Chart calculation is cheap and standard. What u pay for is interpretation and convenience. Some of the most capable jyotish software is free.

If it says Vedic, it is sidereal.

Run the August test. A number of apps labelled Vedic return tropical positions.

All astrology apps are the same product.

Marketplaces, computation tools and AI companions solve different problems. Picking the wrong category is the most common disappointment.

A confident app is a good app.

A system that never says "we cannot know that from this data" is not measuring anything.

Free means the app is not making money from me.

It means the revenue is somewhere else. Find out where before u decide whether u mind.

Reviews on the store tell you which is best.

Store ratings measure satisfaction with the experience, not whether the ayanamsa is correct. Nobody rates an app down for the wrong zodiac.

faq

what is the single most important check on an astrology app?

whether it tells u which ayanamsa it uses. lahiri is the indian government standard. an app that will not say is either using a tropical zodiac or has not thought about it, and both are disqualifying for vedic work.

how do i tell if an app is actually sidereal?

check ur sun sign against ur birth date. if a mid-august birthday still returns leo, it is tropical. in the sidereal zodiac most of august is karka.

are there different kinds of astrology app?

three, and they are barely comparable. consultation marketplaces sell minutes with a human. computation tools sell charts and calculations. ai companions generate readings from ur chart. picking between them is a question about what u actually want.

should an app work without my birth time?

it should tell u honestly what it can and cannot do. sun sign, moon sign, nakshatra and dasha survive without a time. the lagna and all twelve houses do not. an app that silently fills in noon is lying to u by omission.

what ephemeris should it use?

swiss ephemeris is the standard for good reason. what matters more is that the app names its source at all, because an app that will not say usually has nothing worth naming.

is a paid app better than a free one?

not automatically. some of the most capable jyotish software is free desktop software aimed at practitioners. what u are paying for in an app is usually interpretation and convenience, not better maths.

how should an app handle remedies?

carefully, and without selling them. an app that diagnoses a dosha and then routes u to a gemstone it earns commission on has a conflict of interest, whatever the reading says.

what should i check about privacy?

what happens to ur birth details, whether they are shared with third parties, whether readings are processed outside india, and whether u can delete everything from inside the app. under india's DPDP Act u are entitled to ask.

which app is actually the best?

it depends what u want. a human conversation, a computation tool, or an explainer that talks back are three different products. we make the third kind and we will say so plainly rather than pretend to be neutral.

where do other apps beat ask tota?

several places. consultation marketplaces give u a person, which we do not. dedicated jyotish software offers far deeper divisional and dasha tooling. and we are android only right now, so if u are on iphone we are not an option yet.

does an app need to show its calculations?

it should be able to. if u cannot see the degree of every graha, the ayanamsa and the house system, u cannot check anything it tells u, and an unverifiable reading is just prose.

what is a red flag in an astrology app?

urgency. countdown timers on remedies, warnings that a dosha will worsen, and prices that appear immediately after a frightening reading. those are commerce patterns, not astrology ones.
