three ways to find ur moon sign, one of them free

ur rashi is the moon sign, not the sun sign. u can compute it, u can ask ur family, or u can work backwards from the syllable ur name was chosen from. here is all three, with the full nakshatra pada syllable table.

OCTOBER 23, 2026 · 9 MIN READ

every indian family has a rashi for u somewhere, and almost nobody under thirty knows what it is.

it is not ur sun sign. it is the sign the moon was in when u were born, and it is the one used for names, matching, pujas and every dasha calculation there is.

here are three ways to get it, including one that needs no birth details at all because ur parents already did the calculation and encoded the answer in ur name.

the short version

  • ur rashi is the moon sign, computed sidereally. the sun sign is a different measurement and it is not what ur family means.
  • the moon crosses a sign in about 2.25 days, so a birth date usually settles it and the time only matters near a boundary.
  • method one: calculate it. needs date, rough time, place, and lahiri ayanamsa.
  • method two: ask ur family. they have it written somewhere, often on a paper kundli.
  • method three: work backwards from the syllable ur formal name starts with, if it was chosen at namakaran.
  • the reliable cross-check: a computed moon sign should match the rashi ur family has always used.

why it is the moon and not the sun

The Moon moves about 13 degrees a day against the Sun's one. That speed is the whole reason: the Moon distinguishes between people born days apart, while the Sun gives the same answer to everybody born within a month.

and there are two structural reasons stacked on top of the speed.

  • it starts ur dasha. the vimshottari sequence is computed from how far the moon had travelled through its nakshatra at birth. that number decides ur entire 120 year timeline.
  • it is what matching runs on. ashtakoota scores two moons and their nakshatras. the sun does not appear in it at all.

so when a family asks for a rashi, they are asking for the input to two systems that both run on the moon. the moon in ur chart goes through the rest of what chandra carries.

method one: calculate it

Enter your birth date, birth time and birth place into any tool set to the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa. The Moon's position gets computed from ephemeris data and the sign follows directly.

tota says

ur parents almost certainly did this calculation once, at ur namakaran, and then wrote the answer into ur name. that is not sentiment, it is a lookup table, and u can run it backwards.

method two: ask ur family

Most Indian families have the rashi written down somewhere: on a paper kundli made at birth, in a horoscope prepared for matching, or simply remembered by whoever handles the pujas.

this is the fastest route and the most reliable cross-check, because it was usually computed close to the event by someone with the correct birth time in front of them.

what to ask for, in order of usefulness:

  • the paper kundli. often a folded sheet or a small booklet. it will have the rashi, the nakshatra, and frequently the pada.
  • the nakshatra alone. more precise than the rashi and it implies the rashi anyway.
  • the naming syllable. if nobody kept the kundli, somebody usually remembers which syllable the pandit gave.

and if the family answer disagrees with a computed one, the family answer is usually right about the sign, because it was computed from a birth time that has since been rounded or misremembered. that disagreement is a signal to go and find the original record.

method three: work backwards from ur name

One traditional naming method assigns each nakshatra pada a syllable, and the child's formal name is chosen to begin with it. If your namakaran name is known, the table runs backwards from the syllable to a nakshatra, and from there to a rashi.

this is why so many indian families have two names for the same person: a formal one chosen from the chart, and the one everyone actually uses.

the method has real limits and they are worth stating plainly:

  • it only works if the formal name was chosen this way. many were not.
  • a syllable maps to one pada of one nakshatra, but several nakshatras share similar sounds, so u may land on two candidates.
  • transliteration is loose. chu, choo and chü are the same slot.

used as a cross-check rather than a sole source, it is genuinely good. if a computed moon sign and a naming syllable point at the same nakshatra, u can stop worrying about it.

cross-checks, and what goes wrong

Two independent sources agreeing is the standard to aim for. A computed moon sign that matches the rashi your family has always used is settled. If they disagree, something specific is wrong and it is usually one of four things.

and one honest note about the naming method. if ur syllable points at a nakshatra that contradicts both a computed chart and ur family's answer, the likeliest explanation is that ur name simply was not chosen that way. plenty of names are picked because somebody liked them, which is a perfectly good reason.

myths, one at a time

My rashi is my sun sign.

In Indian practice, rashi means the moon sign. It is what names, matching and pujas have always used.

I need an exact birth time to know my moon sign.

The moon spends over two days in a sign, so a date and a rough time usually settle it. Precision matters for the nakshatra and the dasha, not the sign.

My moon sign changes as I get older.

It is fixed at birth. What changes every couple of days is the transiting moon, which is a completely different thing.

The nakshatra is just a subdivision of the rashi.

27 nakshatras across 12 signs is 2.25 each, so nine of them straddle a sign boundary. They are two grids, not one nested inside the other.

Every Indian name encodes a nakshatra.

Only names chosen at namakaran by that method. Many families choose names for entirely ordinary reasons.

If my app disagrees with my family, the app is right.

Usually not, for the sign. The family answer was often computed close to the birth with the correct time, before it got rounded or misremembered.

faq

what is a moon sign?

the sidereal sign the moon occupied at ur birth. in indian practice this is ur rashi, and it is what families mean when they ask for it. the sun sign is a different and much less used measurement.

what do i need to find my moon sign?

ur birth date, a rough birth time and ur birth place. the moon crosses a sign in about 2.25 days, so the date usually settles it and the time only matters near a boundary.

can i find my moon sign from my name?

often, yes. many indian names are chosen from a syllable assigned to the nakshatra pada the moon occupied at birth. if ur formal naming syllable is known, the table works backwards to a nakshatra and from there to a rashi.

what is namakaran?

the naming ceremony. one traditional method picks the first syllable of the name from the moon's nakshatra pada at birth, which is why so many families have a formal name that differs from the one used at home.

why does my app give a different moon sign to my family?

almost always an ayanamsa problem. if the app is set to tropical rather than sidereal, positions land about 24 degrees ahead, which moves the sign. check that lahiri is selected.

how precise does my birth time need to be?

for the sign, a rough time is usually enough. for the nakshatra u want it to the hour, and for the pada or the dasha start date u want it to the minute.

what if i was born near a sign boundary?

then the hour decides it. the moon covers about half a degree an hour, so a placement in the last or first degree of a sign needs a real birth time rather than an estimate.

is the moon sign the same as the nakshatra?

no. there are 12 rashis and 27 nakshatras, so each sign contains two and a quarter nakshatras. the nakshatra is the finer measurement and it is what the dasha is calculated from.

why does indian astrology use the moon and not the sun?

because the moon moves fast, so it distinguishes people born days apart, and because it carries the mind. it also starts the vimshottari dasha and is what kundli matching is scored on.

can i find my moon sign without any birth time at all?

usually yes for the sign, since the moon spends over two days in each one. u may not be able to settle the nakshatra, and u will not be able to compute an accurate dasha start.

what is janma rashi?

the same thing. janma means birth, so janma rashi is the birth moon sign. u will also see chandra rashi used for it.

does the moon sign change over my life?

no. it is fixed at birth. the transiting moon changes sign every couple of days, but ur birth moon sign never moves.
