what venus mahadasha is

Venus mahadasha is the 20 year Vimshottari period ruled by Venus, the longest of the nine. Venus's significations of love, beauty, comfort, art and pleasure, along with the houses Venus rules and occupies in your chart, become the background theme of those years. It follows Ketu's period and precedes the Sun's.

the transition into venus is often striking, because it follows ketu, the planet of detachment and letting go. seven years of ketu's austerity then twenty years of venus's appetite for life is one of the sharpest changes of tone in the cycle.

because it is twenty years, a venus mahadasha can span very different life stages. a venus period from 15 to 35 covers first love, education, career and maybe marriage. from 45 to 65 it covers something else entirely. the planet is the same; the life it lands in is not.

what decides how urs goes

Venus mahadasha results depend on which houses Venus rules from your ascendant, the house it occupies, its dignity (exalted in Pisces, own sign in Taurus or Libra, debilitated in Virgo), combustion, and the planets that join or aspect it. The antardasha lords then shade each stretch.

combustion is worth highlighting. venus never strays more than about 47 degrees from the sun, so it is combust in a noticeable share of charts. that is the single most overlooked factor in venus readings.

where venus is the best planet in the chart

Venus is yogakaraka, ruling both a kendra and a trikona, for Capricorn ascendants (5th and 10th houses) and Aquarius ascendants (4th and 9th houses). For them, Venus mahadasha is traditionally among the best periods of life. For Taurus and Libra ascendants, Venus is the lagna lord and generally supportive.

the nine antardashas

Venus mahadasha divides into nine antardashas: Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury and Ketu. Each lasts 20 times that planet's years divided by 120, which is simply one sixth of the planet's own dasha length, from 1 year for the Sun to 3 years 4 months for Venus.

venus-jupiter is an interesting pair: the two benefics, but also the two gurus, venus of the asuras and jupiter of the devas, who are traditionally neutral to each other rather than friends. the method for reading each pair is in antardasha.

themes people report

Commonly reported themes in Venus mahadasha include relationships and marriage, enjoyment of comfort and beauty, creative and artistic work, social life, vehicles and home improvements, and growing financial ease. The house Venus occupies and rules in your chart decides where these themes concentrate.

  • relationships. venus is the karaka of partnership, so love and marriage often come forward.
  • comfort. better surroundings, nicer things, more attention to how life feels.
  • creativity. art, music, design and style are classic venus territory.
  • money. venus is associated with prosperity and also with spending it.

the shadow side of a benefic

A natural benefic can still give difficult results if it rules dusthana houses, is debilitated or combust, or is afflicted by malefics. In Venus's case, the usual risks are overindulgence, complicated relationships and overspending. Traditional remedies include Friday observances, Lakshmi worship and charity of white items.

the tradition frames venus's challenge as one of balance: enjoying life without being ruled by appetite. that is why its remedies emphasise moderation and generosity, and why the traditional stone, diamond or white sapphire, should never be bought just because the dasha has started. see gemstones by planet.

how long is venus mahadasha?

20 years, the longest of all nine mahadashas. it comes after ketu's 7 years and before the sun's 6.

is venus mahadasha good?

venus is a natural benefic, so its period has a friendly reputation. whether urs is good depends on what venus rules from ur ascendant, its sign and house, and its associations. for capricorn and aquarius ascendants it is the yogakaraka, the most beneficial planet.

what are the effects of venus mahadasha?

commonly reported themes are relationships and marriage, comfort and luxury, art, music, fashion and beauty, vehicles and property, and growth in finances. where venus sits in ur houses decides which themes come forward.

does venus mahadasha bring marriage?

venus is the natural significator of marriage and partnership, so its period often features relationship themes. actual timing depends on ur 7th house, its lord and transits as well.

what is the first antardasha in venus mahadasha?

venus-venus, exactly 3 years 4 months, the longest antardasha in the whole system. then sun, moon, mars, rahu, jupiter, saturn, mercury and ketu.

which antardasha is best in venus mahadasha?

saturn and mercury are venus's natural friends, so venus-saturn and venus-mercury are often read as smoother. venus-saturn is especially valued for capricorn, aquarius, taurus and libra ascendants.

is venus mahadasha good for career?

it often favours careers in arts, design, fashion, hospitality, beauty, entertainment, luxury goods and anything involving relationships with people. venus's house placement refines this.

what does an exalted venus in mahadasha mean?

venus is exalted in pisces. an exalted, well placed venus is traditionally read as giving refinement, comfort and harmonious relationships during its period.

what does a debilitated venus in mahadasha mean?

venus is debilitated in virgo. relationships and pleasures may need more care, unless neecha bhanga applies. check the cancellation rules before drawing conclusions.

can venus mahadasha be difficult?

yes. a venus ruling the 6th, 8th or 12th, or afflicted by malefics, can bring venusian themes through harder routes: overindulgence, relationship tangles or spending. benefic does not mean effortless.

what are the remedies for venus mahadasha?

traditional observances include friday fasting, venus mantras, lakshmi worship, donating white items such as rice or sugar, and respecting women. diamond or white sapphire is the traditional stone, only after chart review.

what comes after venus mahadasha?

sun mahadasha, 6 years. the change from venus's twenty years of relationship and comfort themes to the sun's shorter, more self-directed period is often noticeable.
