what retrograde actually is

Retrograde motion is apparent, not real. Planets always orbit the Sun in the same direction. When Earth overtakes a slower outer planet, or a faster inner planet overtakes Earth, the planet appears to drift backwards against the background stars for a while. For outer planets this happens around opposition, when they are closest and brightest.

u have seen this on a highway. pass a slower car and, for a moment, it seems to slide backwards relative to the hills behind it. it is still going forwards. only ur viewpoint changed.

this gives retrograde an interesting physical meaning. when mars, jupiter or saturn is retrograde, earth is between it and the sun, so the planet is at its nearest and shines brightest in the night sky. the tradition calling these planets strong is, at least, consistent with how they look.

how common it is

Each planet spends a different share of time retrograde: Saturn about 36 percent, Jupiter about 30, Mercury about 19, Mars about 9 and Venus about 7. Combined, roughly seven in ten people have at least one retrograde planet in their birth chart, not counting Rahu and Ketu.

tota says

"retrograde means broken" is a pop astrology idea, not a jyotish one. the classical texts give a retrograde planet full motional strength. think of it as a planet with the volume turned up and the speaker pointed inward.

why the tradition reads it as strength

In shadbala, the classical six-fold strength measure, a retrograde planet receives the maximum chesta bala, or motional strength. Classical authors describe retrograde planets as powerful, with some saying they give results as if exalted. The tradition agrees they are strong and intense, though authors differ on exactly how their dignity changes.

the common modern interpretation blends that strength with the image of backward motion: a retrograde planet expresses itself intensely but inwardly, revisits its themes, and often delivers later or in an unusual way. that is a reasonable synthesis, and it is a long way from "this planet does not work".

where authors disagree is dignity. some say a retrograde debilitated planet behaves more like an exalted one, others that retrogression reverses whatever dignity the planet has. if u see confident one-line rules about this, know it is a debated corner.

Each retrograde planet is read through its own significations turned inward and intensified. how to read urs

Read a retrograde planet like any other, by sign, house, lordship and aspects, then add retrogression as an intensifier: stronger, more inward, more likely to revisit its themes. Its dasha is when the retrograde quality is most felt. Do not read retrogression alone as weakness.

  1. read the planet normally. sign, house, houses ruled, aspects.
  2. add strength. a retrograde benefic is more beneficial, a retrograde malefic more intense.
  3. add inwardness. its themes may be processed internally or revisited.
  4. check its dasha. that is when it speaks loudest.

natal retrograde vs retrograde transits

A natal retrograde planet is a permanent feature of your birth chart. A retrograde transit is a planet's current apparent motion, shared by everyone for a few weeks or months. The two are different: having natal Mercury retrograde does not mean you live in a permanent Mercury retrograde transit.

for the transit side of the story, the famous one has its own explainers: how mercury retrograde works and what it is said to do. some astrologers say people with a natal retrograde planet feel that planet's retrograde transits less, since it mirrors their own chart. that is a popular modern idea rather than a classical rule.

faq

what does a retrograde planet in my birth chart mean?

it means that planet appeared to move backwards against the stars when u were born. classical vedic astrology gives retrograde planets extra strength, called chesta bala, and reads them as intense and inward, expressing their themes in an unusual or repeated way.

is a retrograde planet bad in vedic astrology?

no. classical texts treat retrograde planets as strong. a retrograde benefic is often read as more beneficial and a retrograde malefic as more intense. modern pop astrology's idea that retrograde means broken does not come from jyotish.

why do planets go retrograde?

because earth is also moving. when earth overtakes an outer planet, or an inner planet overtakes earth, the planet appears to drift backwards against the stars for a while, the way a slower car seems to roll backwards when u pass it on a highway.

how common are retrograde planets in a birth chart?

very common. saturn is retrograde roughly a third of the time and jupiter about 30 percent. adding mercury, mars and venus, roughly seven in ten charts have at least one retrograde planet, not counting rahu and ketu.

which planets can be retrograde?

mars, mercury, jupiter, venus and saturn. the sun and moon are never retrograde. rahu and ketu, the lunar nodes, move backwards through the zodiac nearly all the time, so they are treated as always retrograde.

what is chesta bala?

motional strength, one of the six components of shadbala, the classical strength measure. a planet in retrograde motion receives the maximum chesta bala, which is why retrograde planets are read as strong.

what does retrograde jupiter mean in a birth chart?

jupiter is retrograde about 30 percent of the time. it is often read as wisdom turned inward: an independent, questioning relationship with belief, teachers and meaning, sometimes with jupiter's blessings arriving later in life.

what does retrograde saturn mean in a birth chart?

saturn is retrograde about 36 percent of the time. it is often read as a strong inner sense of responsibility and a tendency to revisit duties, structures and old lessons until they are fully learned.

what does retrograde mercury in a birth chart mean?

natal mercury retrograde, roughly one chart in five, is often read as a reflective, non-linear thinker who processes before speaking. it is not the same thing as the transit people blame for broken phones.

does a retrograde planet act like an exalted planet?

some classical authors say a retrograde planet gives results as if exalted, even when debilitated. others say it reverses its dignity. the tradition agrees it is strong, and disagrees on the details.

what happens when a natal retrograde planet turns direct by progression?

progressions are mainly a western technique. in vedic astrology the more common timing tools are the planet's dasha and its transits. a retrograde planet's dasha is when its intensity is most felt.

is natal retrograde the same as mercury retrograde transit?

no. natal retrograde describes ur chart at birth and is permanent. a retrograde transit is the planet's current motion, which affects everyone's sky for a few weeks or months.
