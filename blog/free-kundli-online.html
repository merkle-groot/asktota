every astrology site in india offers a free kundli, and they are not being generous.

a birth chart is a solved computation. the planetary positions come from a standard ephemeris, the rest is arithmetic, and one more chart costs the server a rounding error.

so the chart is not the product. it is the door. here is what is on the other side of it, in the four shapes it usually takes, and what u are actually handing over on the way in.

  • so free kundlis are funded elsewhere: ads, consultation upsell, remedy commission, or freemium.
  • what u pay with is ur birth date, exact time, place, and usually a phone number. that combination is unusually identifying.

  • it is fair to pay for interpretation, a person's time, and product upkeep. not for the calculation.

why the chart itself is nearly free to make

Planetary positions for any moment in history are available from standard ephemeris data. Converting those into a sidereal chart is a fixed sequence of arithmetic. There is no proprietary insight and no expensive step, which is why every site can give it away.

the pipeline is short and everyone runs the same one:

  1. convert the birth date, time and place into universal time and a set of coordinates.
  2. look up the tropical positions of the grahas at that instant from the ephemeris.
  3. subtract the ayanamsa to get sidereal positions.
  4. compute the ascending degree from the location and the local sidereal time.
  5. assign houses. in whole sign, this is trivial.
  6. compute the vimshottari dasha from the moon's position in its nakshatra.

none of that is a trade secret. it is in textbooks, and the hard part, the ephemeris itself, is produced by astronomers and is available to everybody.

which means: if someone is charging u for the chart, they are charging u for a lookup. anything genuinely worth paying for sits downstream of step six.

the four business models

Free kundlis are funded in four ways, usually in combination. Knowing which one you are inside tells you what the site wants from you next.

modelhow it workswhat to watch for
Advertisingthe chart page carries ads, often astrology-adjacentads dressed as readings. the line between content and placement gets thin here.
Consultation upsellthe free chart ends with a prompt to talk to an astrologer, priced per minutea free reading that raises a worry and stops. the worry is the hook.
Remedy commissionthe reading names a dosha, then routes u to a gemstone, yantra or pujathis is the one with a real conflict of interest. the diagnosis and the shop are the same party.
Freemium subscriptionthe chart is free, deeper features are behind a planwhether the limits are visible before u start, or discovered when u hit them.

none of these are illegitimate. products cost money to build and somebody has to pay for it. the third one is the only one with a structural problem, because the party diagnosing the condition also sells the cure, and that is a conflict no amount of sincerity resolves. we went through it in gemstones by planet.

what u are actually paying with

A kundli needs your exact date, time and place of birth. Most sites also ask for a phone number or email. That combination is unusually identifying, and none of it is needed twice.

think about what is in that set. an exact birth timestamp and location is close to a unique key for a person. it is the sort of detail used to verify identity elsewhere, and it does not change, ever.

and notice that a phone number is not required to compute a chart. nothing in the six steps above needs it. it is collected because it lets somebody contact u later, which tells u exactly which of the four models u are inside.

in india this is covered by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. broadly, u are entitled to be told what is being collected and why, and to ask for it to be deleted. the practical questions worth asking any app or site:

  • what exactly is stored, and for how long?
  • is it shared with third parties, and which?
  • is anything processed outside india?
  • can u delete everything, from inside the product, without emailing anyone?

a product that answers those clearly in its privacy policy has thought about it. one that does not, has not.

why two free kundlis disagree

The astronomy is identical everywhere because everyone uses the same ephemeris. Differences come from settings: the ayanamsa, the house system, and how the birth place was resolved to a time zone.

what differswhat u seehow to check
Different ayanamsapositions off by a few degrees, borderline placements fliplook for a settings line saying Lahiri
Tropical, not siderealeverything about 24 degrees ahead, usually a whole signa mid-August birthday returning Leo instead of Karka
Different house systemthe same grahas landing in different houseswhole sign is the vedic standard
Time zone mis-resolvedlagna off by one or two signsconfirm the town resolved to the right zone, especially near borders

run one cross-check that costs nothing: does the moon sign match what ur family has always called ur rashi? indian households use the sidereal moon sign, so a mismatch there is a strong signal that a setting is wrong somewhere.

what u should get for free, and what is fair to charge for

Everything that is arithmetic should be free, because it costs nothing to produce. Everything that is judgement, human time or ongoing maintenance is a real cost and it is reasonable to pay for it.

The chart, in both North and South Indian layoutsInterpretation written for ur specific chart
Every graha with degree, sign and nakshatraA person's time on a call
The lagna and the house layoutOngoing features and product upkeep
Vimshottari dasha with datesDepth: unlimited questions, saved charts, comparisons
The ayanamsa and house system, statedNothing that removes a fear the same product created

that last cell is the line worth holding. paying for depth is normal. paying to undo an anxiety that was manufactured two screens earlier is not a transaction anybody should be running.

the checklist

before u type ur birth details into anything, six questions, about twenty seconds:

  1. does it name the ayanamsa? lahiri, ideally, and visible.
  2. can u see the degrees? not just signs. degrees, minutes, nakshatra, pada.
  3. does it show dasha dates? if not, it is a horoscope generator.
  4. what happens with no birth time? honest tools say the houses are unavailable.
  5. is there a delete option? inside the product, not via email.
  6. does the free reading end in a worry? if it does, u have found the funnel.

and one more that is not on any list: does the moon sign match what ur family calls ur rashi. that single cross-check catches more errors than the other five combined.

myths, one at a time

A paid kundli is more accurate than a free one.

The calculation is standard and cheap. Accuracy depends on the ayanamsa, the house system and ur birth data, none of which improve with price.

Free means nobody is making money from me.

It means the revenue is elsewhere. Ads, consultations, remedies or a subscription. Worth knowing which before u decide whether u mind.

If two sites disagree, one is broken.

Usually both are computing correctly with different settings. Check the ayanamsa first.

Giving a phone number is required for the chart.

Nothing in the calculation needs it. It is collected so u can be contacted later.

Birth details are not sensitive data.

An exact timestamp and location is close to a unique identifier, it never changes, and it is covered by the DPDP Act.

A kundli in hindi is calculated differently.

Language is presentation. The arithmetic is identical. Run the same checks.

faq

why is a kundli free everywhere?

because computing one is genuinely cheap. the planetary positions come from a standard ephemeris and the rest is arithmetic. the marginal cost of one more chart is close to nothing, so nobody can charge for it.

so how do free kundli sites make money?

four ways, usually in combination: advertising, upselling a paid consultation, commission on remedies and gemstones, and a freemium subscription. the chart is the hook, not the product.

is a free kundli accurate?

the astronomy usually is, because everyone is drawing from the same ephemeris data. what differs is the ayanamsa, the house system and how the birth place and time zone were resolved. those are settings, and they change the answer.

why do two free kundlis give me different charts?

different ayanamsa is the most common reason. after that: tropical instead of sidereal, a different house system, or a birth place that resolved to the wrong time zone.

what am i actually giving up for a free kundli?

ur date, exact time and place of birth, and usually a phone number or email. that combination is unusually identifying, and it is worth knowing where it goes.

what does the law say about my birth data?

in india, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 covers it. u are entitled to know what is collected and why, and to ask for it to be deleted. an app with no delete option is worth asking about.

what should i get for free?

the chart itself, every graha with its degree and nakshatra, the lagna, the house layout, and ur vimshottari dasha dates. all of that is arithmetic and none of it is worth charging for.

what is fair to pay for?

interpretation, a person's time, and the ongoing work of maintaining a product. those are real costs. the chart calculation is not.

should i give my phone number to get a kundli?

that is ur call, but notice what it is for. a phone number is not needed to compute a chart. it is needed to contact u later, which tells u what the business model is.

are hindi kundli sites different?

not mathematically. language is a presentation layer. run the same checks: ayanamsa, house system, whether the degrees are visible.

what if i do not know my exact birth time?

a good tool will tell u that the lagna and the houses are unavailable and still give u sun, moon, nakshatra and dasha. one that silently uses noon is handing u twelve confident house placements that are probably wrong.

how do i check a free kundli is right?

cross-check the moon sign against what ur family has always called ur rashi, confirm the ayanamsa is lahiri, and confirm the birth place resolved to the correct time zone. those three catch nearly every error.
