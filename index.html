the fine print

questions people ask before they install

what is ask tota?

ask tota is a vedic astrology app for iphone and android. it files one reading a day for ur birth chart, written like a tabloid, and it shows the planetary placement behind each line so u can check the reasoning instead of taking it on faith.

is this vedic or western astrology?

vedic. tota calculates sidereal positions with the lahiri ayanamsa, which is the standard across india, and prints sanskrit sign names like mesha and vrishabha. ur vedic sun sign usually lands one sign behind the western one u already know.

is ask tota free?

there's a free tier covering the daily edition, ur full birth chart, and a limited number of questions a day. plus and pro raise those limits. prices are set at launch.

do i need my exact birth time?

depends what u want. ur sun barely moves across a whole day, and ur moon shifts roughly every two and a quarter days, so a rough time is fine for both. ur rising sign changes about every two hours, so houses and rising need a birth time good to a few minutes.

how does the compatibility score work?

it runs ashtakoota, which checks eight factors between two charts and adds them up out of 36. tota shows the per-koota breakdown, not just the total, so u can see which check dragged the number down.

is there an ai writing my readings?

a language model writes the prose. the astrology underneath it is calculated, not generated: real ephemeris positions, real houses, real dashas. tota is not allowed to make a claim it can't attach to a placement, which is why every line has a receipt on it.

when does it launch?

it's headed to the app store and google play. leave ur email above and u get one message on launch day.

who is tota?

tota is the parrot who runs the paper. tota is also just the hindi word for parrot, and in indian street astrology a trained parrot picks the card that holds ur reading. the sunglasses were his idea.
