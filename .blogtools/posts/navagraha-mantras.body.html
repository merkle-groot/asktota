what a beej mantra is

A beej, or seed, mantra is a short sequence of sacred syllables associated with a deity or planet. Each graha's beej mantra combines three seed syllables and the syllable "sah" with the planet's name and "namah", a salutation. They are recited in repetition, usually counted on a mala of 108 beads.

the structure is consistent and easy to learn once u see it. take the sun: om hraam hreem hraum sah suryaya namah. the three syllables share a sound (hraa, hree, hrau), then "sah", then "to the sun, salutations". every planet follows the same pattern with its own seed sound.

the nine beej mantras

The nine beej mantras follow one pattern. Sun: om hraam hreem hraum sah suryaya namah. Moon: om shraam shreem shraum sah chandraya namah. Mars: om kraam kreem kraum sah bhaumaya namah. Saturn: om praam preem praum sah shanaischaraya namah. The others follow the same form.

days, deities and counts

Each graha is linked to a weekday and a deity: the Sun to Sunday and Surya, the Moon to Monday and Shiva, Mars to Tuesday and Hanuman, Mercury to Wednesday and Vishnu, Jupiter to Thursday and Brihaspati or Dakshinamurti, Venus to Friday and Lakshmi, Saturn to Saturday and Hanuman or Shani, Rahu to Durga and Ketu to Ganesha.

one prayer for all nine

The Navagraha shloka addresses all nine planets at once: "brahma murari tripurantakari bhanuh shashi bhumisuto budhashcha, gurushcha shukrah shani rahu ketavah sarve grahah shanti kara bhavantu." It asks Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva, and the nine grahas, to bring peace.

if u do not know which planet to focus on, or simply want a short daily prayer, this is the one most people use. it names the sun (bhanu), moon (shashi), mars (bhumisuta, son of the earth), mercury, jupiter (guru), venus (shukra), saturn, rahu and ketu, and ends with a request for peace from all of them.

which one to chant

Astrologers usually recommend the mantra of your current mahadasha lord, your ascendant lord, or a planet that is weak but important in your chart. If you are unsure, the Navagraha shloka is a safe general practice. Start simple.

  • ur dasha lord. the planet running ur current mahadasha.
  • ur lagna lord. the ruler of ur ascendant, the planet of the self.
  • a weak key planet. debilitated or combust, but ruling an important house.
  • a transit focus. many people chant saturn's mantra during sade sati.

how to start, simply

Choose one mantra. Sit at the same time each day, ideally on the planet's weekday to begin. Use a mala of 108 beads, recite clearly and steadily, and continue for a set period such as 40 days. Keep it simple, regular and unhurried.

  1. pick one mantra. not nine.
  2. pick a time. morning is traditional, but consistency beats timing.
  3. use a mala. 108 beads; a rudraksha mala is common.
  4. commit to a period. 40 days is a traditional unit.
  5. notice how u feel. calmer and steadier is a good sign.

faq

what is a beej mantra?

a seed mantra: a short sacred syllable or set of syllables associated with a deity or planet. each graha's beej mantra combines a seed syllable with the planet's name, for example om hraam hreem hraum sah suryaya namah for the sun.

what is the surya beej mantra?

om hraam hreem hraum sah suryaya namah. it is traditionally recited on sundays, ideally at sunrise.

what is the shani beej mantra?

om praam preem praum sah shanaischaraya namah. it is traditionally recited on saturdays.

what is the chandra beej mantra?

om shraam shreem shraum sah chandraya namah, traditionally recited on mondays.

how many times should i chant a planet mantra?

for daily practice, 108 times on a mala is the traditional unit. commonly cited full japa counts, such as 23,000 for saturn or 7,000 for the sun, are spread over many days. consistency matters more than volume.

which day is for which planet?

sunday sun, monday moon, tuesday mars, wednesday mercury, thursday jupiter, friday venus, saturday saturn. rahu is usually propitiated on saturday and ketu on tuesday or saturday.

which planet mantra should i chant?

astrologers usually suggest the mantra of ur current dasha lord, ur lagna lord, or a weak but important planet in ur chart. if unsure, the general navagraha shloka covers all nine.

can anyone chant navagraha mantras?

yes. mantra japa is generally considered open to anyone and, unlike gemstones, is not traditionally considered risky. correct pronunciation and a sincere, regular practice are what traditions emphasise.

do i need a guru to chant beej mantras?

some traditions say beej mantras are most effective when received from a teacher. many people chant them without formal initiation. the general navagraha shloka and simple name mantras are widely chanted by everyone.

what is the navagraha shloka?

a short prayer to all nine planets: brahma murari tripurantakari bhanuh shashi bhumisuto budhashcha, gurushcha shukrah shani rahu ketavah sarve grahah shanti kara bhavantu. it asks all nine grahas to bring peace.

do mantras really work?

there is no scientific evidence that mantras change planetary influences. there is research suggesting repetitive mantra or meditation practice can reduce stress for some people. many value it as devotion and discipline.

are mantras better than gemstones?

traditionally mantras are considered safe for everyone and free, while gemstones are prescribed cautiously. if u are choosing a remedy, mantras are the gentlest place to start.
