venus gets twenty years, the longest run in the system

shukra carries partnership in every chart regardless of gender, holds the longest dasha of any graha, and like mercury can never get far from the sun. here is what venus actually runs, why it is exalted in meena, and what it does in each house.

OCTOBER 12, 2026 · 11 MIN READ

venus gets twenty years of dasha. that is a fifth of the entire hundred and twenty year cycle handed to one graha.

saturn gets nineteen, rahu eighteen, mercury seventeen. the sun gets six. shukra runs longer than anything, which means for a great many people venus is not an influence in their life, it is a whole era of it.

here is what it actually carries, and why it can never get far from the sun.

twenty years, and what that means

In the Vimshottari system, Venus receives 20 years, the longest allocation of any graha. Across a 120 year cycle that is exactly one sixth of the total, and it is longer than Saturn's 19 and Rahu's 18.

grahadasha length
Venus20 years
Saturn19 years
Rahu18 years
Mercury17 years
Jupiter16 years
Moon10 years
Mars, Ketu7 years each
Sun6 years

the practical consequence is that a venus mahadasha is rarely a chapter. it is usually the whole book. twenty years is a career, a marriage, a city and a set of friends, and it is long enough that people describe it as who they were rather than what happened to them.

which also means the antardashas inside it matter more than usual. twenty years is far too wide to say anything useful about on its own, and the sub-periods are what actually narrow it. the dasha system covers how those nest.

what shukra actually carries

Venus signifies love, partnership, beauty, art, taste, comfort, luxury and vehicles. The through-line is enjoyment: what you find pleasant, what you want around you, and who you want beside you.

the cleanest way to separate venus from the moon: the moon is what settles u, venus is what delights u. comfort and pleasure are not the same, and charts where the two disagree are unusually interesting to read.

the second leashed graha

Venus orbits inside Earth's orbit, so from here it can never appear more than about 47 degrees from the Sun. It is always within two signs of it. Only Venus and Mercury have this constraint.

this has three consequences worth knowing, and they mirror mercury's 28 degree leash at a wider radius.

  • venus is often near the sun, so it combusts reasonably often. the commonly used orb is 10 degrees, or 8 when retrograde, which is narrower than mercury's, so it burns less often than mercury does.
  • venus and the sun are natural enemies in the friendship tables, and yet they are permanently forced to stand close together. the tradition noticed that tension and it shows up in readings about desire versus duty.
  • venus is the morning and evening star. the brightest object in the sky after the sun and moon, and always near the horizon at dawn or dusk. that visibility is part of why it carries beauty.

and one small practical note: because venus can be at most two signs from the sun, a chart where venus and the sun are three or more signs apart has been calculated wrongly. it is a free error check.

meena and kanya

Venus is exalted in Meena at 27 degrees and debilitated in Kanya at 27 degrees. Meena is Jupiter's sign of sympathy and dissolved boundaries. Kanya is Mercury's sign of analysis and correction.

meena is the sign where edges soften and separations blur. a graha whose entire business is attraction and union is most itself where the boundary between people is thinnest. venus in meena is the least conditional form of love the system describes.

kanya is the opposite environment, and the reasoning is worth stating precisely. kanya's competence is noticing what is wrong with things. that is genuinely useful, and it is the hardest possible setting for a graha built on acceptance and enjoyment.

the honest reading of a debilitated venus is not "unlovable". it is a person who sees the flaw first and has to consciously decide to enjoy something anyway. that is a real pattern, it is common, and neecha bhanga cancellations apply to it like everything else.

and note the symmetry across the system: mercury is exalted in kanya, where venus is debilitated. the sign that rewards precision penalises acceptance. the design keeps being consistent.

the karaka for partnership, in every chart

Venus is the kalatra karaka, the significator of the spouse, and this applies in every chart regardless of gender. The classical texts additionally weight Jupiter for the husband in a woman's chart, which is a convention of those texts rather than a symmetry in the system.

it is worth being precise about this because the popular version gets it wrong in both directions.

what the karaka system says: venus signifies partnership, for everyone. it is not a "feminine planet" assigned to women. it is the graha of attraction and union, and everybody has one.

what the classical texts add: jupiter is weighted for the husband in a woman's chart. that is a real convention in the source material and it should be named as one, from a period with particular assumptions, rather than quietly presented as timeless.

what actually gets read for partnership: the 7th house, the 7th lord, venus, and the dasha sequence. venus is one of four inputs and it is never read alone. when will i get married goes through the whole method.

jupiter's opposite number

In the puranic story Shukracharya is the guru of the asuras, opposite Brihaspati who teaches the devas. Read as a frame rather than a morality tale, it is one of the more useful pairings in the system.

the two great benefics are set up as teachers of different things. jupiter teaches doctrine: what is right, what is owed, what the framework says. venus teaches desire: what is wanted, what is beautiful, what is worth having.

both are legitimate. a life organised entirely by jupiter is dutiful and joyless. a life organised entirely by venus is pleasant and unmoored. the tradition gives u two advisers rather than one, and expects u to argue with both.

that reading also explains the friendship tables neatly. jupiter counts venus as an enemy and venus counts jupiter as neutral, which is exactly what u would expect from a relationship where one side thinks the other is frivolous and the other thinks the first is a bit much.

myths, one at a time

Venus is the woman's planet.

Venus is the karaka for partnership in every chart. Assigning grahas to genders is an import, not a feature of the karaka system.

A debilitated venus in kanya means an unhappy love life.

It describes seeing the flaw first and choosing to enjoy something anyway. Common, and neecha bhanga cancellations apply.

A strong venus guarantees a good marriage.

Venus is one of four inputs, with the 7th house, the 7th lord and the dasha. It is never read alone.

Venus is always helpful because it is a benefic.

It can rule difficult houses for ur lagna, and a natural benefic ruling a kendra is weakened by kendradhipati dosha.

Venus far from the sun means something special.

It cannot get more than about 47 degrees away. If a chart shows more than that, the chart is wrong.

A 20 year venus dasha is 20 good years.

It is 20 years with a recognisable flavour. What it delivers depends on venus's condition and what it rules for ur lagna, and the antardashas do most of the actual work.

faq

what does venus represent in vedic astrology?

love, partnership, beauty, art, taste, comfort, luxury and vehicles. shukra is the kalatra karaka, the significator of the spouse, in every chart regardless of gender.

which signs does venus rule?

vrishabha and tula. its mooltrikona is tula from 0 to 15 degrees. it is exalted in meena, deepest at 27 degrees, and debilitated in kanya, deepest at 27 degrees.

how long is venus mahadasha?

20 years, the longest of any graha in vimshottari. that is a fifth of the whole 120 year cycle, which is why a venus period tends to define an entire era of someone's life.

how far can venus get from the sun?

about 47 degrees at most, so it is never more than roughly two signs away. venus and mercury are the only grahas with that constraint, because both orbit inside earth's orbit.

why is venus exalted in meena?

meena is jupiter's sign of sympathy, imagination and dissolved boundaries. a graha built for love and beauty is most itself where the edges between people are softest.

why is venus debilitated in kanya?

kanya is mercury's sign of analysis, correction and usefulness. a graha built for enjoyment and acceptance is least comfortable in the sign that inspects everything for faults.

which nakshatras does venus rule?

bharani, purva phalguni and purva ashadha. if ur moon was in one of those at birth, ur vimshottari dasha sequence started with venus.

which house is venus strongest in?

the 4th, by directional strength, which it shares with the moon. digbala puts venus at full force in the house of home, comfort and the emotional base.

is venus a benefic?

yes, the second greatest natural benefic after jupiter. but it can rule difficult houses for a given lagna, and a natural benefic ruling a kendra is weakened by kendradhipati dosha.

does venus mean the same thing in a man's and a woman's chart?

venus is the karaka for partnership in every chart. the classical texts additionally weight jupiter for the husband in a woman's chart. that is a convention of those texts and worth naming as one rather than repeating unexamined.

what does venus retrograde mean?

venus retrogrades for about 40 days roughly every 19 months, so a smaller share of people are born with it than with mercury retrograde. the classical scoring treats a retrograde graha as gaining motional strength.

why is venus the guru of the asuras?

shukracharya is the teacher of the asuras in the puranic story, opposite brihaspati who teaches the devas. it is a useful frame: jupiter is doctrine and venus is desire, and both are legitimate teachers of something.
