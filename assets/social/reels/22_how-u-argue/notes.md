## the share mechanic

This is the only reel in the set built to leave the app. The closer asks for a
comment **and** a tag, because an argument reel is the one people send to
somebody rather than just reply to. A DM share is worth more than a like and it
is the only signal here that reaches someone who does not follow the account.

`ur group chat, by graha` (reel 14) is the other share-shaped reel. Same rule:
do not schedule them in the same week, they compete for the same behaviour.

## the line that keeps it honest

"none of these are the good one." Mars is a tool and the twelve versions are
twelve tools. A reel that ranks placements reads as a horoscope column and
invites the one comment nobody wants, which is people asking whether theirs is
bad.
