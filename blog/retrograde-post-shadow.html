THE BLOG · THE RETROGRADE DESK

the retrograde ended. why does nothing feel finished?

mercury stations direct on 13 november and the internet declares it over. it isn't, because the same fifteen degrees are still being crossed for a third time. here is what a shadow period actually is, and how much of it to believe.

SEPTEMBER 18, 2026 · 9 MIN READ

mercury stations direct on 13 november and half the internet posts a celebration graphic.

then the week after arrives and the thing u were waiting on is still not resolved, the reply still has not come, and the decision u parked is still parked.

there is a mechanical explanation for the first part of that, a psychological one for the second, and it is worth being able to tell them apart.

the short version

  • the shadow is the slice of zodiac a retrograding planet crosses three times: forwards, backwards, forwards.
  • the post-shadow runs from the direct station until the planet finally passes the degree where it first stationed retrograde. in 2026 that is 13 to about 30 november.
  • so the "it's over" moment is not the end of anything geometric. the same degrees are still being walked over.
  • the shadow framing is modern and western in origin. classical vedic vocabulary has vakri and atichari and does not build a three phase model.
  • with shadows counted, the full cycle is about nine to ten weeks, three times a year. that is a third of the calendar under caution, which is too much.
  • the honest use: close the loops u opened. that works whether or not the astrology does.

what a shadow period actually is

When a planet retrogrades, it does not reverse across new territory. It walks back over a stretch of zodiac it has already covered, then walks forward over it again. That thrice-crossed stretch is the shadow, and the two phases either side of the retrograde are the pre-shadow and the post-shadow.

the picture is simple once u draw it on a line rather than in the sky.

mercury moves forward through the signs. at some point it stops and turns, then walks backwards for about three weeks, then stops again and resumes forwards. the stretch it walked backwards over is territory it had just covered, and territory it is about to cover again.

so three passes over the same degrees. that is the entire idea, and everything else people say about shadow periods is interpretation layered on top of that one geometric fact.

worked, with degrees

Note the degree where the planet stations retrograde, and the degree where it stations direct. Everything between them is the shadow. The pre-shadow begins when the planet first reaches the direct-station degree, and the post-shadow ends when it finally passes the retrograde-station degree.

use round numbers. say mercury stations retrograde at 20 degrees of a sign and stations direct at 5 degrees of the same sign.

fifteen degrees of sky, crossed three times, over roughly nine weeks. the retrograde is only the middle third of that.

and this is why the direct station is not a reset. on 13 november mercury turns around, and it still has fifteen degrees to re-cover before it is anywhere it has not already been twice. that takes another two to three weeks. the mechanics post covers where those dates come from.

tota says

notice how the window keeps growing. three weeks becomes nine once shadows are added, and nine becomes twenty seven across a year. any framework that expands to cover a third of ur life has stopped describing events and started describing the calendar.

it is a modern idea, and that is fine to say

The shadow-period framing comes out of modern western astrological practice. Classical Vedic astrology has words for retrograde motion and fast motion and treats retrograde as a strength factor, but it does not construct a three-phase model around a retrograde window.

this matters because of how the idea usually reaches people, which is with the word ancient attached to it.

the vedic vocabulary is vakri for retrograde and atichari for unusually fast. a vakri graha earns cheshta bala, motional strength, in the shadbala calculation. that is the classical treatment, and it is about scoring a graha's force in a chart rather than about mapping out phases of a transit.

none of which makes the shadow idea worthless. the geometry is real, and dividing a cycle into three passes is a reasonable thing to do with it. it just should not be presented as something parashara wrote down.

why nothing feels finished

Two ordinary reasons, and neither needs the sky. Three weeks of deferred decisions do not clear in a day. And a label that promises a reset sets up an expectation the calendar was never going to meet.

if u genuinely spent three weeks postponing things because the internet told u to, then on day twenty two u have three weeks of backlog. it will take another few weeks to clear. that is arithmetic, not astrology.

and if u did not postpone anything, u still absorbed a fortnight of content telling u that a boundary was coming. boundaries create expectations. an expectation of relief that is not met reads as still stuck, even when nothing is.

the frequency illusion runs in this direction too. once u are watching for post-shadow friction, u will find it, at exactly the rate u would have found it in any other three weeks. we went through that machinery in what mercury retrograde actually correlates with.

the third-of-the-year problem

Retrograde alone is about 18 percent of the year. Add the pre-shadow and post-shadow, as the advice usually does, and the cautioned window climbs toward a third of the calendar.

what u countdays per yearshare of the year
Retrograde only~60-65~18%
Plus post-shadow~110-120~32%
Plus pre-shadow too~160-180~45%

at the bottom of that table, nearly half of every year is under a caution. at that point the advice is not guidance, it is a way of never deciding anything, and nobody who repeats it actually lives that way.

which is the honest test for any framework: if following it fully would be absurd, then nobody is following it fully, and it is worth asking what it is really for.

what the window is genuinely good for

Closing loops. The post-shadow is a useful, culturally recognised prompt to finish what got reopened, send the follow-up, and sign what got deferred. That is a scheduling habit, and it works on its own terms.

  • finish the reopened thing. whatever came back round in the last three weeks, close it now rather than letting it drift into december.
  • send the follow-up. the one u drafted and did not send. everybody has one.
  • sign what u deferred. if u actually postponed something on retrograde advice, put a date on it this week.
  • do not start a new caution. when the post-shadow ends, the next pre-shadow is not far away. this can absorb a whole year if u let it.

that is the whole of the useful advice. it is small, it is free, and it does not require anybody to believe anything.

myths, one at a time

The direct station is a reset.

Geometrically it is the start of the third pass over the same degrees. Nothing resets; the planet just turns around.

Shadow periods are ancient Vedic doctrine.

The framing is modern and western. Classical Vedic astrology has vakri and atichari and treats retrograde as a strength factor.

You should wait out the whole shadow before deciding anything.

That is up to 45 percent of the year. Following it fully would be absurd, and nobody actually does.

Only mercury has shadow periods.

Every retrograding planet does, by the same geometry. Nobody tracks saturn's, and saturn is retrograde for more of the year.

Feeling unsettled after it ends proves the shadow is real.

Three weeks of deferred admin takes weeks to clear, and an unmet expectation of relief reads as being stuck. Both are ordinary.

There is a remedy to shorten the post-shadow.

It is a planetary position on a published schedule that applies to everybody at once. It ends when the ephemeris says it ends.

faq

what is the post-shadow period?

the stretch after a planet stations direct, while it re-crosses the degrees it had already covered during the retrograde. it ends when the planet finally passes the degree where it originally stationed retrograde.

when does the 2026 mercury post-shadow end?

around 30 november. mercury stations direct on 13 november and then takes a further two to three weeks to clear the degrees it had already walked over.

why is it called a shadow?

because the planet passes through the same slice of zodiac three times: forwards, backwards, then forwards again. that thrice-crossed slice is the shadow, and it is the same degrees on all three passes.

what is the pre-shadow?

the mirror image at the front. it starts when the planet first crosses the degree it will later retrograde back to, and runs until the retrograde station.

is the shadow period a classical vedic idea?

no. the shadow framing is modern, and it comes out of western astrological practice. the classical vedic vocabulary has vakri for retrograde and atichari for fast motion, and does not build a three-phase model around it.

so why do people still feel unsettled after it ends?

mostly because three weeks of deferred admin does not clear itself in a day. things reopened during the window take time to close, and that is ordinary scheduling rather than sky mechanics.

how long is the whole cycle including shadows?

roughly nine to ten weeks per retrograde. three of retrograde, and about three either side. across three windows a year that is a large fraction of the calendar, which is worth noticing before treating it as a caution.

should i wait until the post-shadow ends to make decisions?

if u count shadows, u are cautioning about a third of every year. no working tradition ever asked people to defer a third of their lives, and following it would cost u far more than any transit ever has.

does the direct station feel like anything?

the station is the part practitioners weight most, because the planet sits on one degree for days instead of sprinting through a sign. whether u notice it is a separate question and one there is no evidence for.

what is a good use for the post-shadow window?

closing loops. finish the thing u reopened, send the follow-up, sign what u deferred. that is a scheduling habit, and it works whether or not the astrology does.

do other planets have shadow periods?

the same geometry applies to every retrograding planet, so yes. nobody tracks saturn's shadow, even though saturn is retrograde for more of the year than mercury.

how do i know which degrees the shadow covers?

note the degree where the planet stations retrograde and the degree where it stations direct. everything between those two points is the shadow, and it gets crossed three times.
