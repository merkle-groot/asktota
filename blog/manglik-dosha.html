manglik dosha is a house number, not a sentence

mars in one of six houses. that is the whole definition. here is how common it really is, why two astrologers can give u opposite answers about the same chart, which cancellations the old texts list, and what is being sold to u in its name.

AUGUST 13, 2026 · 12 MIN READ

few sentences in indian astrology land as hard, with as little explanation, as "she's manglik". it gets said like a diagnosis, and nobody ever says what was measured.

so here is the measurement. manglik dosha means Mars was in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house of ur chart. that is the whole thing.

everything else, the severity, the marriage warnings, the remedies, is meaning piled on top of one house number. this post pulls the two apart.

the short version

  • manglik dosha, also called mangal dosha, kuja dosha or chevvai dosham, means Mars sits in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house.
  • it is common. six houses out of twelve is half the chart, before u even get to how astrologers count. estimates land anywhere from a quarter to a half of everyone.
  • astrologers count from different starting points: ur rising sign, ur moon, or ur Venus. that alone explains most of the disagreements about one chart.
  • the old texts list cancellations, called mangal dosha bhanga, and they apply often. a proper reading checks those before saying anything.
  • the strongest point against the scary version: if both people are manglik, the tradition says it cancels out. a flaw that fixes itself when u double it is a matching rule, not a curse.
  • no controlled study has ever linked manglik status to how marriages turn out. treat scary claims with a price tag exactly as suspiciously as they deserve.

what manglik dosha actually is

Manglik dosha is the label for Mars sitting in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house of a birth chart. Those six are grouped together because the old texts tie them to the body, family, home, marriage, upheaval and loss. All places Mars is thought to stir things up.

in this tradition Mars runs nerve, temper, competition, physical energy and anything sharp. it is a natural malefic, which means difficult and demanding, not evil.

the six houses are not a random pick. here is the reasoning:

1stbody, temperamenta short fuse aimed at the partner
2ndfamily, speech, moneysharp speech, friction with the extended family
4thhome, emotional basedomestic peace disturbed
7thmarriage itselfconflict placed directly in the marriage house
8thupheaval, longevity, joint assetsthe most severe reading, historically tied to widowhood fears
12thexpenditure, the bed, separationwithdrawal and distance in the marriage

whatever u make of that reasoning, notice what it is. a rule about position, producing a category. it does not measure anyone's temper, predict an event, or say a thing about anyone's character.

how common is it really

Six houses out of twelve is half the chart. If Mars landed evenly, counting from the rising sign alone would flag about half of everyone. Real distributions and stricter definitions bring the usual quoted figures down to about a quarter or a third, which is still huge.

this is the number that should change the whole conversation, and nobody mentions it when they tell someone they are manglik.

start with the arithmetic. the dosha needs Mars in six of the twelve houses. that is half the places it could be.

count from the rising sign alone and u are flagging something close to half of everyone. stricter versions that drop the 2nd house, or that require no cancellation to apply, bring it down. published estimates land between a quarter and a half depending whose definition u use.

now think about what happens when u count from three starting points instead of one, which plenty of astrologers do. check from the rising sign, the moon and Venus, and call someone manglik if any one of them qualifies, and the share goes up a lot. each extra count is another chance to land in six houses out of twelve.

none of this means the tradition is wrong to have the category. it means a label that fits somewhere between a quarter and a half of everyone alive is a group, not a diagnosis.

if a third of the people u know are manglik, and a third of them are happily married, the scary reading has some explaining to do.

why two astrologers give u opposite answers

Because they count from different starting points and use different lists of houses. One counts Mars from ur rising sign, another from ur moon, a third from Venus. North indian practice usually counts the 2nd house, some southern practice does not. Same chart, different method, different answer.

this is the most useful thing to know before anyone makes a pronouncement about ur chart. it explains nearly every case of two astrologers disagreeing about one person.

the reference point

counting from the rising sign is the most common. counting from the birth moon is a well established second method. counting from Venus, because Venus stands for marriage, is used by some schools.

Mars can easily land in a counted house from one starting point and miss from another. our calculator prints all three, because picking one and calling it the answer would be pretending a real disagreement is settled.

the house list

almost everyone counts the 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th and 12th. the 2nd house is argued over. north indian practice usually counts it, several southern traditions do not.

that one house is the whole difference between manglik and not, for a lot of people.

the severity grading

some astrologers grade by house, calling the 7th and 8th serious and the 2nd and 12th mild. others treat all six the same. that is a judgment call, not a rule, and almost nobody says so out loud.

so if someone tells u ur manglik, ask two things. counted from what? and does ur list include the 2nd house? those two answers will tell u more than any amount of talk about what it means.

the cancellations, which nobody mentions first

The old texts list conditions under which manglik dosha does not apply. They are called mangal dosha bhanga. They include Mars in its own sign or exalted, Jupiter looking at the 7th house, Mars sitting with certain planets, and both partners being manglik.

these cancellations come from the same texts that define the dosha in the first place. a reading that names the dosha and stops there has done half the job.

the ones in common use:

  • Mars in its own sign, or exalted. Mars in Mesha, Vrishchika or Makara is usually read as the dosha standing down, because a planet working somewhere it likes is not the one causing trouble.
  • Mars in Karka or Simha. several schools treat these as neutralising, though not all agree.
  • Jupiter aspecting or occupying the 7th house. the classical great benefic looking after the marriage house is one of the most commonly cited cancellations.
  • Mars conjunct or aspected by Saturn, Rahu or Ketu. treated as tempering by several sources.
  • both partners manglik. the best known cancellation and the most revealing one.
  • Mars in specific house and sign combinations, for example Mars in the 7th in Makara or Karka, which classical sources list individually.

the sources do not fully agree on this list either, and we would rather say so. what they all agree on is that cancellations exist and are not rare.

so any reading that jumps straight from the label to a remedy has skipped a step the tradition itself asks for.

the both-manglik rule, and what it gives away

Classical practice says that if both partners are manglik, the dosha cancels and the match is fine. This is the most revealing rule in the whole subject, because a real flaw in one person cannot be cured by the same flaw in another.

sit with that for a second. if manglik dosha were something genuinely wrong with a person, something that damaged marriages, then putting two of them together should do twice the damage.

it does not. and the tradition says so itself. two mangliks are considered a good match, and the dosha is treated as cancelled.

which tells u what the rule really is. it is not a statement about a person. it is a matching rule: a way of pairing people with similar charts, inside a system built to sort through a lot of introductions quickly. read it that way and it makes sense, and it stops being frightening.

it also leaves the sales version in an awkward spot. if two mangliks marrying is fine, then being manglik is not a fault that needs an expensive repair. it is a preference to match around, like any other.

what is actually being sold

The manglik label is one of the most heavily sold ideas in indian astrology. The pattern never changes: name the fear, hint that a marriage is at risk, then offer a paid fix. The label itself is free, and anyone can work it out from a chart.

we would rather say this plainly than leave it hanging.

the sales pattern is easy to spot once u know it. the reading opens with how serious it is, usually about marriage. it never mentions how common the dosha is. it never says which starting point was used. it never checks the cancellations. and a specific priced remedy turns up in the same conversation.

the most heavily marketed one is kumbh vivah, a ceremonial marriage to a pot, a tree or an idol, said to soak up the dosha. it is a real folk practice with real regional history.

it is also sold at prices that have nothing to do with folk practice, to people who were never told the dosha fits a huge share of the population.

three questions worth asking before u pay anyone anything:

  1. counted from the rising sign, the moon or Venus? and does ur list include the 2nd house?
  2. which cancellations did u check, and what did they show?
  3. what happens if I do nothing?

an honest astrologer will answer all three without getting annoyed. those same three answers are exactly what a sales script cannot survive.

so what should u actually do about it

Find out which starting point was used, check the cancellations, and treat the label as one thing on a list rather than a verdict. If the manglik question is being used to push u around, the problem is the pushing, not the chart.

practical steps, in order:

  1. get the actual placement. which house is Mars in, counted from where. a real number, not a label. the calculator above gives it to u free.
  2. check the cancellations. if one applies, the tradition itself says the question is closed.
  3. keep it in proportion. ashtakoota scores 36 points across eight checks, and manglik is not one of them. it is a separate box, and one item on a long list.
  4. separate the astrology from the pressure. a lot of the time, the chart is being used as a polite way to say something else. the chart is not the real conversation.
  5. do not pay to fix a category. if a remedy shows up before anyone has told u the starting point and the cancellations, ur being sold to.

and if u have just found out ur manglik: somewhere between a quarter and a half of the people u know are too. most of them have no idea. it has not visibly wrecked their lives.

what gets said about manglik dosha, and what is actually the case

A manglik person will harm or outlive their spouse.

This is the harshest old reading of Mars in the 8th house, and it does more damage than anything else in this subject. Nobody has ever tested it. It does not follow from the rule about houses. And it is the claim used most often to justify an expensive remedy.

Being manglik means I cannot marry a non-manglik.

The tradition says such a match needs more care. It never says forbidden, and it lists several cancellations that close the question outright. Millions of these marriages already exist.

Manglik dosha is rare and serious.

It means Mars is in six of twelve houses. The usual quoted rates run from a quarter to a half of all charts, depending on method. Something that broad cannot also be rare.

Every astrologer will give me the same manglik answer.

They will not, and everyone inside the practice knows it. They count from different starting points, and they disagree about whether the 2nd house belongs on the list.

Kumbh vivah is a required fix.

It is a regional folk practice with real history, and nothing about it is required. Selling it to people who were never told how common the dosha is deserves every bit of suspicion u can give it.

Doing a remedy removes the dosha from my chart.

The chart never changes. Mars is where it was at the minute u were born and it stays there for good. What a remedy claims to change is the effect, which is a completely different kind of claim.

mistakes worth not making

  • taking the label without asking where it was counted from. rising sign, moon or Venus changes the answer, and it is a fair question to ask.
  • skipping the cancellations. they are part of the classical method, not a loophole someone made up to be nice.
  • thinking manglik is part of the 36 point score. it is not. it is a separate check that runs alongside.
  • paying for a remedy before u know the placement. if the price arrived before the house number did, that is the tell.
  • letting a chart carry a conversation that is really about something else. if manglik only came up after some other objection, the chart is not the subject.

the receipt

the house list, the cancellations and the both-manglik rule are classical. they come from the standard treatment of kuja dosha, and from how Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra handles Mars and the 7th house. the regional argument about the 2nd house is real and documented in practice, not something we are claiming.

the base rate arithmetic is ours, and it is simple. six houses of twelve is half the chart, before u count from any extra starting point. the quoted range of a quarter to a half reflects the different definitions in circulation. we give a range rather than one number, because one number here would be a fake kind of precision.

the calculator counts Mars from ur rising sign, ur birth moon and ur Venus, using whole sign houses and the Lahiri ayanamsa, and reports all three instead of picking. planet positions come from JPL Keplerian elements, accurate to a few arcminutes between 1800 and 2050.

we know of no controlled study testing manglik status against how marriages turn out. we do not think one exists, and we would rather say that than hint at evidence we cannot point to.

frequently asked questions

what is manglik dosha?

it is the label u get when Mars sits in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house of ur birth chart. it also goes by mangal dosha, kuja dosha or chevvai dosham.

how do I know if I am manglik?

find which house Mars is in, counted from ur rising sign. plenty of astrologers also count from ur moon and ur Venus. the calculator above shows all three.

how common is manglik dosha?

very. six houses out of twelve is half the chart, and the usual quoted rates run from about a quarter to a half, depending which definition u use.

can two mangliks marry?

yes. classical practice says the dosha cancels out completely. that is the tradition's own rule, and it tells u a great deal about what the category really is.

can a manglik marry a non-manglik?

yes. the tradition says it needs more care, not that it is forbidden, and it lists several cancellations that close the question. plenty of these marriages exist already.

what cancels manglik dosha?

the usual ones are Mars in its own sign or exalted, Jupiter sitting in or looking at the 7th house, Mars next to Saturn or the nodes, and both partners being manglik.

is the 2nd house counted for manglik dosha?

it depends who u ask. north indian practice usually counts it. some southern practice does not. that one disagreement decides the answer for a lot of people.

which house is the worst for manglik dosha?

the 7th and 8th get the harshest readings. the 7th because it is the marriage house, the 8th because of old associations with how long people live. many astrologers grade by house, but the grading is a judgment call, not a fixed rule.

does manglik dosha affect anything other than marriage?

the classical claims are almost all about marriage and home life. wider claims about ur career or health are not standard.

is manglik dosha in the ashtakoota score?

no. ashtakoota scores 36 points across eight checks, read from the two birth nakshatras. manglik is a separate check that runs alongside.

what is kumbh vivah?

a ceremonial marriage to a pot, a tree or an idol, said to soak up the dosha before a real wedding. it is a genuine regional folk practice, and also one of the most oversold remedies in the whole category.

does manglik dosha reduce with age?

some astrologers say the effect fades after a certain age, usually given as 28. that is a traditional claim, not something calculated, and the sources disagree about the number.

is there evidence manglik dosha affects marriages?

no controlled study has ever tested it. it is a traditional category for reading a chart, and we would rather say so plainly than let the silence suggest otherwise.

what should I do if I am told I am manglik?

ask what it was counted from. ask which cancellations were checked. ask what happens if u do nothing. those three questions tell u whether ur getting a reading or a sales pitch.

does a remedy remove manglik dosha from my chart?

the chart never changes. Mars stays exactly where it was at the minute u were born. what remedies claim to change is the effect, which is a different kind of claim and worth thinking about clearly.

the five things worth remembering

  1. manglik dosha means Mars in one of six houses. that is everything the label actually measures.
  2. it fits somewhere between a quarter and a half of all charts, which makes it a group, not a diagnosis.
  3. astrologers disagree because they count from different starting points and different house lists. always ask which.
  4. the both-manglik cancellation gives the game away. a flaw that fixes itself when u double it is a matching rule, not a curse.
  5. if a price shows up before the house number does, stop and start asking questions.
