every western compatibility chart agrees: aries and leo are a dream match. fire and fire, big energy, great chemistry.

vedic astrology looks at the same pair and finds a dosha. not because it dislikes passion, but because it measures compatibility differently. it uses moon signs, not sun signs, and it scores the distance between them, not their element. by that rule, aries and leo are a 5/9 pair, and 5/9 scores zero on bhakoot.

why vedic uses moon signs

Vedic astrology judges compatibility from the Moon because the Moon represents the mind, emotions and daily experience, which is what living together involves. The Moon's sign and nakshatra are the basis of the Ashtakoota matching system. Sun sign compatibility is a Western convention, built on a different zodiac and different rules.

this is the first reason vedic and western compatibility give different answers: they are often not even comparing the same signs. ur vedic moon sign might be nowhere near ur western sun sign. if u do not know urs, start with finding ur moon sign.

the distance rule

Bhakoot koota counts from one Moon sign to the other and back. Pairs that are the same sign, or 3/11, 4/10 or 7/7 apart, score the full 7 points. Pairs 2/12, 5/9 or 6/8 apart score zero, which is bhakoot dosha. Six of the twelve possible distances fall on each side.

where vedic and western disagree

Western astrology rates trines, signs of the same element four signs apart, as the most harmonious pairing. In Vedic bhakoot, those same pairs are 5/9, which scores zero. Western astrology also treats oppositions as tense, while the Vedic 7/7 opposition scores full marks. The systems use different logic and reach different answers.

the second check: sign lords

Graha maitri, worth 5 points, scores the natural friendship between the lords of the two Moon signs. If the lords are friends, the score is high; if enemies, low. It often confirms or softens bhakoot, and same-lord or friendly-lord pairs are a common basis for cancelling bhakoot dosha.

example: cancer and pisces are a 5/9 pair, bhakoot dosha. but their lords, the moon and jupiter, are natural friends, so graha maitri is high and many astrologers would cancel or soften the bhakoot dosha. the friendship table is in planetary friendships.

what matters more than the table

Sign-level checks are a small part of Vedic matching. A full reading uses all eight kootas, including the nakshatra-based ones, then compares both charts' 7th houses, Venus, Mars, dashas and overall strength. And no controlled research shows any compatibility system predicts relationship success.

faq

how does vedic astrology judge zodiac compatibility?

mainly from the two people's moon signs and moon nakshatras, through the ashtakoota system. at the sign level, the key koota is bhakoot, which scores the distance between the two moon signs, along with graha maitri, the friendship between their sign lords.

should i use my sun sign or moon sign for compatibility?

in vedic astrology, ur moon sign. the moon represents the mind and emotions, which is what compatibility is about. western sun sign charts follow a different system and often give different answers.

which moon sign pairs are most compatible in vedic astrology?

on bhakoot, pairs that are the same sign, 3 and 11 apart, 4 and 10 apart, or 7 apart (opposite signs) score the full 7 points. the 7/7 relationship is often read as especially complementary.

which moon sign pairs are least compatible?

pairs 2 and 12 apart, 5 and 9 apart, or 6 and 8 apart score zero on bhakoot. the 6/8 relationship, shadashtak, is traditionally treated as the most challenging.

why are aries and leo incompatible in vedic astrology?

leo is the 5th sign from aries, so a moon pair in aries and leo is a 5/9 relationship, which is bhakoot dosha. western astrology calls the same pair a harmonious fire trine. the two systems simply use different rules.

is aries compatible with libra in vedic astrology?

on bhakoot, yes. libra is the 7th sign from aries, a 7/7 opposition, which scores the full 7 points. their lords, mars and venus, are neutral to each other in graha maitri.

are same moon signs compatible?

same sign scores the full 7 on bhakoot, and the sign lord is shared, so graha maitri is full too. other kootas like nadi can still raise issues if both moons are in the same nakshatra.

what is graha maitri in compatibility?

the 5 point koota measuring the natural friendship between the lords of the two moon signs. friends score high, enemies low. it is a separate check from bhakoot and often softens or confirms it.

is moon sign compatibility enough to judge a relationship?

no. sign level checks are a small part of matching. a fuller reading looks at all eight kootas, then at both charts' 7th houses, venus, mars, dashas and overall strength. and no chart decides a relationship for u.

can bhakoot dosha be cancelled?

yes. it is commonly cancelled when both moon signs share a lord, such as aries and scorpio under mars, and often when the two lords are friends. see the bhakoot dosha guide for details.

which zodiac sign is the best match for each sign?

each moon sign has six signs that score full bhakoot and six that do not. the table in this article lists them. treat it as a starting point, never a verdict.

does compatibility astrology actually work?

no controlled research shows sign or koota compatibility predicts relationship success. many families use it as a traditional starting point for conversation. communication, values and kindness are better predictors.
