## the pick is the mechanic

**2 to 1, kohli.** Dhoni is the sentimental favourite in almost every room this
reel will land in, so scoring it against him is the entire point. A verdict that
agrees with the timeline gets likes. A verdict that contradicts it gets replies,
and replies are what keep a reel moving.

No. 002 also scored 2 to 1 against the expected side. That is now a pattern and
it needs watching: if every head to head picks the underdog, the format stops
being a judgement and becomes a gimmick people can predict. **No. 005 should
score with the consensus** if the charts support it, or go 3 to 0, or the trick
wears out.

The downfall desk is round four and has no winner, same as no. 002. Both weak
spots are one sentence read in two directions: the calm that saves is the calm
that hides, the fire that drives is the fire that burns. A tie after three scored
rounds is what makes the scoreline read as judged rather than rigged.

## the rules, unchanged

Character and chart only. Never a claim about a real person's private life,
health or conduct. Public birth dates and no birth times, said on the cold open,
in the case header and in the caption. Entertainment, said out loud.

"The desk leans here" is a verdict on two charts. It is never a verdict on two
people, and on a rivalry this hot the distinction is the only thing keeping the
comments survivable.
