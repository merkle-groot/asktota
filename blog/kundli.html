ur kundli is a diagram, not a prophecy

twelve boxes, nine grahas, one minute of one day. here is what each part of a kundli is, why the north and south indian versions look nothing alike, and how to read yours in the order that actually works.

AUGUST 1, 2026 · 13 MIN READ

someone hands u a kundli and it looks like a homework diagram drawn by an architect who was in a hurry. boxes, numbers, two-letter abbreviations, and no legend.

there is a legend. a kundli is a scale drawing of the sky above ur birthplace at ur birth minute, flattened into twelve compartments. once u know which compartment is which and what the shorthand means, u can read the structure of any chart in about ninety seconds. this piece gives u that, and a calculator that will draw ur own so u have something real to practise on.

the short version

  • a kundli is ur Vedic birth chart. same thing as a janam kundali, janampatri, or natal chart.
  • it needs three inputs and will not work properly without all three: date, time and place of birth.
  • the twelve boxes are houses, not signs. house 1 starts at ur rising sign and the rest follow in order.
  • north Indian charts keep the houses in fixed positions and move the signs. south Indian charts keep the signs in fixed positions and move the houses. same data, two layouts.
  • read it in this order: lagna, lagna lord, moon and nakshatra, then the rest. not the other way round.
  • the diagram is arithmetic. what it means about u is interpretation. those are different claims and worth keeping apart.

what a kundli actually is

A kundli is a two-dimensional map of the sky as it was from ur birthplace at ur birth minute. It shows where the nine grahas stood against the sidereal zodiac, arranged into twelve houses counted from whichever sign was rising on the eastern horizon at that moment.

strip the ornament away and a kundli is a table of nine numbers, each between 0 and 360 degrees, plus one more number for the horizon. everything else on the page is a way of drawing those ten numbers so a human can hold them in their head.

the word comes from the Sanskrit kundala, a coil or ring, which describes the circular sky it flattens. it is the same object a western astrologer calls a natal chart, computed against a different zero point and drawn in a different shape.

a kundli is not a fortune. it does not update. it is fixed the moment u are born and it never changes again, which is why the tradition builds all of its timing on top of it with dashas and transits rather than inside it.

the three things it needs, and why

A kundli needs ur date, time and place of birth. Date fixes the sun and roughly the moon. Time fixes the moon precisely and the rising sign entirely. Place fixes the horizon, because the sign rising in Kochi at 6am is not the sign rising in Delhi at 6am.

the reason astrologers ask for birth time so insistently is not ceremony. without it the entire house grid is unplaceable, and houses are where the specifics live. a chart without a birth time can still tell u ur moon sign, ur nakshatra and ur dasha sequence, which is genuinely useful, but it cannot tell u which house anything is in.

if ur birth time is missing or unreliable, say so out loud rather than guessing. a guessed time produces a chart that is precisely, confidently wrong, and every conclusion built on it inherits the error. how accurate does ur birth time need to be has the tolerances worked out placement by placement.

north indian vs south indian: same chart, two drawings

North Indian kundlis keep the twelve houses in fixed positions on the page and write the sign number into each box. South Indian kundlis keep the twelve signs in fixed positions and mark which box is the ascendant. The underlying data is identical. Only the drawing convention differs.

this trips up more beginners than any other part of the subject, because the two charts look like completely different documents.

north indian style

a square rotated into a diamond grid. the houses stay put: the top centre diamond is always house 1, and u count anticlockwise from there. what changes chart to chart is the sign number written inside each box, from 1 for Mesha to 12 for Meena. so u read the shape for the house and the number for the sign.

south indian style

a fixed four-by-four square with the middle punched out. the signs stay put: Mesha is always in the same position, and every chart in the world has Meena in the same corner. the ascendant is marked, usually with a diagonal line, and u count houses clockwise from there.

east indian or bengali style

a third layout, again a square, drawn with diagonals. less common outside Bengal, Odisha and Assam, and read on the same principle.

none of these is more accurate than the others. if two astrologers give u different readings it is not because one used a diamond and one used a square. our calculator prints a plain table instead of any of the three, because a table is easier to check and impossible to misread.

how to read a kundli, in order

Read a kundli in this order: the lagna, then the lagna lord and where it sits, then the moon and its nakshatra, then which houses hold planets, then dignity and combustion, and only last the yogas and doshas. Reading it in any other order produces confident nonsense.

most bad chart reading is not a knowledge problem, it is an order problem. people start at the dramatic end and work backwards.

  1. find the lagna. ur rising sign. this decides which sign sits in which house, so it decides everything downstream. an hour of error here and u are reading someone else's chart.
  2. find the lagna lord and where it went. whichever planet rules ur rising sign is the closest thing ur chart has to a protagonist. its house placement is the single most informative fact after the lagna itself.
  3. find the moon, its sign and its nakshatra. the moon is the mind in this system, and its nakshatra sets ur dasha sequence. in Indian usage ur moon sign is ur rashi, the one people mean when they ask.
  4. see which houses are occupied. nine grahas cannot fill twelve houses, so five or six empty houses is normal and means nothing bad. an empty house is read through its lord instead.
  5. check dignity and state. is a planet in its own sign, exalted, debilitated, combust, retrograde? this is condition, not verdict. a debilitated planet usually reads as effort, not failure.
  6. only now look at yogas and doshas. combinations. and read two sources on any dosha before u accept a frightening version of it.

a chart read in that order is boring for the first four steps and then suddenly specific. a chart read backwards is exciting immediately and usually wrong.

the abbreviations, decoded

Kundlis use two-letter shorthand for the grahas and often mark condition next to them. Su is Sun, Mo is Moon, Ma is Mars, Me is Mercury, Ju is Jupiter, Ve is Venus, Sa is Saturn, Ra is Rahu, Ke is Ketu. An R next to a planet means retrograde.

why one chart is not enough: divisional charts

The main chart, the rashi or D1, is the body of the reading. Vedic astrology also generates divisional charts, or vargas, by subdividing each sign. The D9 navamsa, used for marriage and inner strength, and the D10 dashamsha, used for career, are the two u will meet most.

the divisional charts are one of the things that make jyotish structurally different from western practice. u take the same nine positions and slice each 30 degree sign into finer units, then rebuild a whole new chart from which slice each planet landed in.

the practical consequence is precision pressure. the navamsa divides each sign into slices of 3 degrees 20 minutes, so a four minute error in ur birth time can move a planet into a different navamsa box. that is the real reason accurate birth time matters more here than in western astrology, where nothing subdivides that finely.

an old rule of thumb worth keeping: if a promise appears in the D1 but collapses in the D9, treat it as unconfirmed. divisional charts are used to check the main chart, not to replace it.

kundli matching is a different job

Kundli matching, or guna milan, compares two birth nakshatras across eight kootas and scores the pair out of 36. It is a compatibility screen, not a reading of either chart. A high score does not describe either person, and a low score is a prompt to look closer rather than a verdict.

worth separating clearly, because the phrase gets used for both. reading a kundli means interpreting one person's chart. matching kundlis means running two charts through the ashtakoota scoring system to produce a number out of 36.

the eight kootas are weighted unevenly, and the heaviest single one, Nadi, carries 8 of the 36 points on its own. the usual family threshold is 18 out of 36, which is a convention rather than anything the classical texts fix. we pulled the whole scoring apart, koota by koota, in how ashtakoota actually scores compatibility out of 36.

one thing worth knowing before anyone quotes a score at u: guna milan reads only the two moons and their nakshatras. it does not look at ur 7th house, ur Venus, ur Mars, or either person's dasha. it is a fast screen built for arranged introductions at scale, and treating its number as a full compatibility verdict asks it to do a job it was never designed for.

what gets repeated, and what is actually true

A kundli predicts what will happen in my life.

It records where nine objects were at one moment. Prediction is something an interpreter adds on top, using dashas and transits. The diagram itself makes no forecast.

A north Indian chart and a south Indian chart can give different results.

They cannot. They are two drawing conventions for identical data. If two readings differ, the reason is the interpreter, the birth time, or the ayanamsa, never the layout.

Empty houses in my kundli mean those parts of life are blank.

Nine grahas cannot occupy twelve houses, so most charts have five to seven empty ones. An empty house is read through its ruling planet, which is sitting somewhere else in the chart doing the work.

A kundli can be generated accurately from just my date of birth.

You can get the sun sign and a rough moon sign that way. You cannot get the rising sign, and without the rising sign there is no house structure, which means no career house, no marriage house, nothing specific.

If two people are born the same minute in the same city they will live the same life.

Their charts will be effectively identical. Their lives are not, which is the strongest available argument against reading a chart as a script rather than as a pattern.

mistakes worth not making

  • rounding ur birth time to the nearest half hour. the lagna moves about a degree every four minutes. rounding can move ur entire house grid.
  • using a chart generated with a different ayanamsa without noticing. Lahiri, Raman and Krishnamurti differ by a degree or two, which is enough to move a planet near a sign boundary.
  • reading houses from ur moon sign and ur lagna interchangeably. both are valid techniques, but they answer different questions and mixing them mid-sentence produces mush.
  • treating an exalted planet as automatically good. dignity describes conditions, not outcomes, and a well-placed planet ruling difficult houses is a more complicated story.
  • going straight to the doshas. almost every chart contains something that can be named alarmingly. start with structure.

the calculator above computes the sun by Meeus geometric solar longitude, the moon by a low-precision lunar series accurate to a few tenths of a degree, and Mars through Saturn from JPL Keplerian elements valid for roughly 1800 to 2050. the ascendant comes from local sidereal time, birth latitude and the obliquity of the ecliptic, and has been checked against the sunrise identity, where the ascendant must equal the sun's longitude at the moment of sunrise, at five latitudes in both hemispheres, agreeing to within 0.12 degrees.

frequently asked questions

what is a kundli?

ur Vedic birth chart. a diagram showing where the nine grahas stood across twelve houses at the exact moment and place u were born.

what is the difference between a kundli and a horoscope?

a kundli is the chart itself. a horoscope in common usage means a daily sun sign prediction, which is a completely different and much shallower thing. confusingly, horoscope in its technical sense means the same as kundli.

how do I get my kundli?

u need ur birth date, birth time and birth place. the calculator on this page will draw one in ur browser. for anything u are making a decision on, use full chart software or a practitioner.

can I get a kundli without a birth time?

partly. u can still get ur moon sign, ur nakshatra and ur dasha sequence, which is a lot. u cannot get ur rising sign, and without that there is no house structure.

what is the most important part of a kundli?

the lagna, ur rising sign. it decides which sign occupies which house, so every other reading depends on it being right.

what is the difference between north and south indian kundli?

drawing convention only. north Indian charts fix the houses and move the signs. south Indian charts fix the signs and move the houses. the data underneath is identical.

why are some houses in my kundli empty?

because there are nine grahas and twelve houses. five to seven empty houses is completely normal. an empty house is read through its ruling planet instead.

what does R mean next to a planet in my kundli?

retrograde: the planet appears to be moving backwards through the zodiac from earth. it is an apparent effect of relative orbital speed, not the planet reversing.

what is a navamsa chart?

the D9 divisional chart, built by dividing each sign into nine parts. it is read for marriage, partnership and a planet's underlying strength, and is usually consulted alongside the main chart rather than instead of it.

is my kundli the same as my western natal chart?

same birth moment, different zero point. the planets are in the same physical places. the sign labels usually shift back by one because of the roughly 24 degree ayanamsa.

how accurate does my birth time need to be for a kundli?

for the main chart, within about ten minutes is comfortable. for divisional charts like the navamsa, which slice each sign into 3 degree 20 minute pieces, even four minutes can matter.

can two people have the same kundli?

two people born in the same minute in the same city have effectively the same chart. their lives still differ, which is the honest limit of what a chart can claim.

what is kundli matching?

a separate procedure that scores two people's birth nakshatras against each other out of 36 points. it reads only the moons, not the full charts.

does a kundli change over time?

no. it is fixed at birth and never updates. what changes is which dasha period u are in and where the planets are transiting now.

is it bad to see my own kundli?

no. it is a diagram of where things were. the only harm in the vicinity comes from someone using it to frighten u into buying something.

the five things worth remembering

  1. a kundli is ten numbers drawn as a picture. date, time and place produce it, and all three are load-bearing.
  2. the lagna is the keystone. get it wrong by two hours and every house in the chart shifts, taking every conclusion with it.
  3. north and south Indian layouts are drawing conventions, not different systems. the data underneath is the same.
  4. read in order: lagna, lagna lord, moon and nakshatra, occupied houses, dignity, and only then yogas and doshas.
  5. empty houses are normal and meaningless on their own. nine planets cannot fill twelve rooms.
